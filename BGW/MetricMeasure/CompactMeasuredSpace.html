    Represented compact metric probability spaces #

    The carrier, its genuine metric, compactness, and Borel probability measure are bundled together. Nonempty carriers are explicit. The Borel condition prevents a separate measurable structure from changing the intended measure.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/CompactMeasuredSpace.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).

    source
    structure BGW.MetricMeasure.CompactMeasuredSpace :
    Type (u + 1)

    A nonempty compact metric space equipped with a Borel probability measure. Carriers lie in one fixed universe; the type of all represented spaces lies in the next universe. No full-support assumption is imposed.

    • carrier : Type u

      The whole underlying space, including points outside the measure support.

    • metric : MetricSpace self.carrier

      The metric structure on the carrier.

    • nonempty : Nonempty self.carrier
    • compact : CompactSpace self.carrier
    • measurable : MeasurableSpace self.carrier

      The measurable structure, required below to be the Borel structure.

    • borel : BorelSpace self.carrier
    • law : MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure self.carrier

      The probability measure on the carrier, without a full-support requirement.
