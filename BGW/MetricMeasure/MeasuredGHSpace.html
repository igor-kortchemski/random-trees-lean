    The GHP topology on measured isometry classes #

    The equivalence relation identifies the entire compact metric space by a measure-preserving isometry, with the whole carrier retained. The ambient-isometry GHP infimum descends to this quotient and satisfies the pseudometric axioms. Its topology and Borel structure are therefore available for weak convergence. Separation, completeness, and separability are separate background results; none is assumed in this construction or the deterministic-limit argument.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/MeasuredGHSpace.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
