    Triangle inequality for the GHP infimum #

    Two ambient realizations are glued isometrically along the whole middle space. The pushed middle measures and images then coincide. The Lévy–Prokhorov and Hausdorff triangle inequalities bound the summed cost, and approximation of the infima gives the GHP triangle inequality.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPTriangle.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
