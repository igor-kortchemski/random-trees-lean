    Joint continuity of positive spatial scaling #

    Two positive scalings of one represented compact probability space are compared using their common original carrier and probability law. The distortion is the difference of factors times the original diameter. The common-source gluing estimate therefore gives the rooted GHP bound with constant 3 / 2.

    Combining this estimate with the fixed-factor Lipschitz bound proves joint continuity on positive factors and rooted measured isometry classes. The argument fixes a representative only at the point of continuity; it does not introduce a global choice of representatives or a diameter observable on the quotient. Probability masses are unchanged throughout.

    source
    theorem BGW.MetricMeasure.RootedCompactMeasuredSpace.distance_scale_scale_le (c d : ℝ) (hc : 0 < c) (hd : 0 < d) (X : RootedCompactMeasuredSpace) :
    (scale c hc X).distance (scale d hd X) ≤ 3 / 2 * |c - d| * Metric.diam Set.univ

    Changing the scaling factor on one represented space is controlled by its diameter.
