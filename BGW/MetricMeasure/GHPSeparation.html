    Separation for the exact measured GHP distance #

    Near-zero actual ambient costs give approximate maps between the entire compact carriers. A compact uniform limit is a surjective isometry. Uniform closeness in the original ambient realizations, together with their vanishing Prokhorov costs, proves that this isometry preserves the probability measures. Thus no points outside the measure supports are discarded.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPSeparation.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
