    Compactness transfer from GH to GHP #

    Hausdorff-precompact full carriers can be put in a common compact hull. Probability compactness on that hull gives a measured convergent subsequence, regardless of the original measures. The limit retains the entire Hausdorff carrier. Consequently the inverse image of a compact GH set under the underlying-space projection is compact for the exact GHP metric.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPCompactness.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
