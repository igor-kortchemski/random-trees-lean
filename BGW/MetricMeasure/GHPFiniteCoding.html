    Countable data for finite measured metric spaces #

    A labelled finite carrier is encoded by rounded pairwise distances and rounded atomic masses. Equal codes give a measure comparison and a bijection of small distortion, hence a bound for the exact GHP distance. Zero-mass points are included in both arrays.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPFiniteCoding.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
