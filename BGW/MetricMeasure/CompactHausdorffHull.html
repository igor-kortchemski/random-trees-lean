    A compact hull for a convergent sequence of compact sets #

    A convergent sequence of compact sets and its limit fit inside a single compact set. The hull retains every point in every set; it is not a measure support. This lets varying compact carriers share one compact probability space in the GHP completeness argument.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/CompactHausdorffHull.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
