    Compact limits of approximate isometries #

    Maps with uniformly vanishing distance distortion and asymptotically dense images have a uniformly convergent refining ultrafilter whose limit is a surjective isometry. The approximating maps need not be continuous or measurable. Compactness and the vanishing distortion prove the uniform limit; no exact-isometry or measure-preservation conclusion is assumed.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/ApproximateIsometryLimit.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).

    ∃ (U : Ultrafilter ι), ↑U ≤ l ∧ ∃ (e : X ≃ᵢ Y), TendstoUniformly f ⇑e ↑U

    Compactness supplies a refining ultrafilter and a genuine surjective isometry as its uniform limit. No continuity of the approximants is required.
