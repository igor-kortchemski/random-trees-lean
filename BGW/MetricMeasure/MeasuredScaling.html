    Positive spatial scaling of unrooted measured GH classes #

    The same representative scaling used for rooted compact probability spaces descends to the unrooted whole-carrier measured-isometry quotient. Measure transport is unchanged. As in the rooted case, GHP estimates use max factor 1 because the Lévy–Prokhorov component is not homogeneous under spatial scaling.
