    Underlying Gromov--Hausdorff classes of rooted measured spaces #

    Forgetting the root and the measure is continuous, and therefore transports weakly convergent probability laws. The declarations remain in their historical Coding namespace for API compatibility, although they do not depend on continuum-tree coding.
