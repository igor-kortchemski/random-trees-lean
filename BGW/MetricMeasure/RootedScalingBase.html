    Positive rescaling of a metric carrier #

    ScaledMetric c X is a fresh carrier with distance multiplied by the positive number c. Its topology and measurable structure are transported from X. MetricSpace.ofDistTopology makes the metric induce that very topology. No metric instance on the original carrier is changed.

    The two quantitative helpers control Hausdorff distance on compact sets and Lévy–Prokhorov distance under Lipschitz maps. The latter uses the factor max K 1, since the probability-error term is not spatially rescaled.
