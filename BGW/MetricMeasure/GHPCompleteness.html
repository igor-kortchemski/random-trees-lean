    Completeness of the whole-space GHP metric #

    Fast successive couplings are realized in one complete ambient space. Their compact images have a Hausdorff limit and all lie in a compact hull. Pushed probability measures have a weakly convergent subsequence on that hull; its ambient pushforward is carried by the limiting compact set. Restriction produces a probability on that entire set. This gives a GHP limit and hence completeness, without requiring full support or separability of the initially constructed ambient space.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPCompleteness.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
