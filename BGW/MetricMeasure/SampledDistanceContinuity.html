    GHP continuity of the actual finite iid distance-matrix laws #

    The compactness extraction is retained together with its fixed compact ambient sampling measures. Their weak convergence gives every finite matrix law by product continuity. For a GHP-convergent sequence, uniqueness identifies every subsequential limit, giving continuity of the quotient observable. Neither distinct samples nor full measure support is assumed.
