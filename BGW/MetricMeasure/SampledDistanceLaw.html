    Actual finite iid sampled-distance laws #

    The law is the pushforward of the full product probability measure by the literal distance matrix. Repeated sampled points remain possible. Measured isometries preserve the law, so it is defined on the source whole-space measured-isometry quotient. On a fixed metric carrier it varies continuously with the sampling probability measure.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Probability/SampledDistanceLaw.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
