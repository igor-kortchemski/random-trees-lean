    Quantitative comparison of pushed probability measures #

    Uniformly close random points have close laws in the Lévy–Prokhorov metric. A nonexpansive map contracts this metric, and weak convergence on a separable metric space implies convergence in Lévy–Prokhorov distance.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Probability/ProkhorovBounds.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
