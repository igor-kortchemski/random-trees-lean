    Forgetting the measure contracts GHP to GH #

    Every measured ambient cost contains the ordinary Hausdorff cost of the same isometric embeddings. Thus the exact Gromov--Hausdorff distance is bounded by the exact measured infimum. In particular zero GHP distance forces a whole-space isometry; preservation of the measure is a further obligation.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPUnderlyingGH.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
