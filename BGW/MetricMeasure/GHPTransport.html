    Transport of the GHP infimum by measured isometries #

    Precomposition by a surjective isometry preserves each ambient cost, including the pushed measure and the entire embedded image. Hence the infimum is invariant under measure-preserving isometry equivalences and symmetric in its arguments.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPTransport.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
