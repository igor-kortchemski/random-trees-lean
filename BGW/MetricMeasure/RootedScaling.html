    Positive spatial scaling of rooted compact probability spaces #

    Only distances are multiplied by the positive factor. Probability measures and roots are transported by the canonical equivalence to ScaledMetric. The GHP comparison uses max factor 1; the probability-error part of the Lévy–Prokhorov metric is not homogeneous under spatial scaling.
