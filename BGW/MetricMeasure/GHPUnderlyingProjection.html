    The continuous underlying GH projection #

    Forgetting the measure is well-defined on the source whole-space measured isometry classes. The actual GH≤GHP inequality makes this projection nonexpansive, so it is continuous and Borel measurable. No assumption about Polishness or the Gromov--Prokhorov quotient is needed.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPUnderlyingProjection.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
