    A common complete ambient space for successive GHP couplings #

    Glue each next coupling along the entire current carrier, form the metric inductive limit, and take its completion. Each original coupling embeds isometrically in the resulting space. This is a geometric construction; the probability measures are subsequently transported through those embeddings.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPChainGluing.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
