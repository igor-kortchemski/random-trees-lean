    The metric quotient of rooted compact probability spaces #

    The equivalence relation is a single whole-carrier isometry preserving both the probability measure and the distinguished point. The actual rooted GHP infimum descends to a metric because its root-preserving separation theorem has already been proved. Its topology and Borel structure are induced by this one metric instance. Completeness and separability are not asserted here.

    Forgetting the root maps to the existing unrooted quotient and is 1-Lipschitz. This construction follows the existing unrooted quotient in BGW.MetricMeasure.MeasuredGHSpace, adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/MeasuredGHSpace.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
