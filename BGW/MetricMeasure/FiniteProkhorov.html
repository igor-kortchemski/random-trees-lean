    Finite atom bounds for Prokhorov distance #

    The difference on any event is bounded by the sum of the absolute differences of its atomic masses. This gives a Prokhorov bound without a lower bound on nonzero masses or on distances between points. Quantizing each atom at a scale divided by the number of points yields countable approximation data.

    Adapted from Freezing2/Probability/FiniteProkhorov.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0).
