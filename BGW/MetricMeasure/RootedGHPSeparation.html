    Separation for rooted measured GHP distance #

    Vanishing actual rooted costs give approximate maps of the whole compact carriers. Their uniform isometric limit preserves the probability measure by the ambient Prokhorov bounds. The additional root-distance bounds show that this same limit sends the first root to the second root.

    The proof extends BGW.MetricMeasure.GHPSeparation, adapted from Freezing2/Geometry/GHPSeparation.lean in igor-kortchemski/uniform-attachment-freezing-scaling-limits, commit 8bace72d36cdf7d250414f3d98acfaa415de23d9 (Apache-2.0). There is no full-support assumption and no assumed root-preserving isometry.

    Zero rooted distance supplies actual ambient realizations of arbitrarily small rooted cost.
