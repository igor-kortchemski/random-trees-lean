    The Vervaat image of the constructed Brownian bridge #

    The anchored Brownian bridge gives a measurable continuous loop for every outcome on the constructed source probability space. Applying the deterministic Vervaat transformation gives an actual probability law on nonnegative continuous paths with zero endpoints.

    The rooted measured tree law is the genuine image under coding by twice this path. Its root is represented by time zero, and its mass is the existing Lebesgue pushforward from the coding construction. The normalization is therefore explicitly T_(2e).

    These are laws of the constructed Vervaat path and its coded tree. Almost-sure uniqueness of the bridge minimizer, strict interior positivity, and identification with the standard Brownian excursion and classical Brownian CRT laws are separate, unproved probabilistic statements.
