    Finite plane trees as rooted compact metric probability spaces #

    The carrier is the existing subtype PlaneTree.Vertex t of present addresses, with its graph metric. Its Borel probability measure gives mass 1 / t.size to every vertex, including the root and all leaves. No measurable structure on the type of plane trees or on all addresses is introduced.

    This module only bundles the finite tree. Metric scaling and comparison with a continuously coded tree are separate constructions.
