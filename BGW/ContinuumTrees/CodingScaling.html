    Spatial scaling of continuously coded trees #

    Multiplying a coding function by a nonnegative constant multiplies its pseudodistance by that constant. For a positive constant, this identifies the coded tree with the spatially scaled original tree by a genuine isometry preserving the root and the Lebesgue pushforward mass.

    This is an identity between scaled spaces. It does not assert homogeneity of GHP distance between two spaces, whose Prokhorov term also measures mass error.
