    +# From uniform contour convergence to rooted measured tree convergence

    The distance divisor tends to infinity and its ratio to the number of vertices tends to zero. Uniform convergence of the contours, normalized to duration one and divided by that divisor, then implies rooted GHP convergence of the vertex spaces with uniform mass on all vertices. No probabilistic invariance principle is assumed to have been proved by this deterministic result.
