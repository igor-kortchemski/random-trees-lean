    The deterministic Vervaat transformation #

    A continuous real function on the unit interval has a first global minimizer. This canonical choice is measurable, including for functions with several minimizers: its sublevel sets are the closed sets on which the minimum over an initial interval equals the global minimum.

    For a continuous loop, rotating time to this minimizer and subtracting the minimum gives a continuous nonnegative function with both endpoints zero. The resulting map on the subtype of continuous loops is measurable. Uniqueness of a Brownian bridge's minimizer, strict interior positivity, and identification of the transformed law with a Brownian excursion are separate probabilistic statements, not conclusions of this deterministic construction.
