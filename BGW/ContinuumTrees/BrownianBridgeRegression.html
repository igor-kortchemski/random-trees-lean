    Regression on a Brownian bridge increment #

    Subtracting the covariance profile times one increment leaves zero covariance with that increment at every time. The profile is explicit on the intervals before and after the increment, which will separate their minimum values. The normalized identities retain nondegeneracy of the increment. Joint Gaussianity then proves independence of the continuous residual path and the increment, using exactly the sigma-algebra generated by path evaluations. Uniqueness of minima is a later probability result.
