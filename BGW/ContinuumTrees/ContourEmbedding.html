    Isometric embedding of vertices in their contour-coded tree #

    A vertex is represented by its first contour visit. The minimum between two such visits is their common ancestor's depth, so the coding distance agrees with the graph metric. The embedding preserves the root. Its mass comparison with the contour's Lebesgue pushforward is a separate result.
