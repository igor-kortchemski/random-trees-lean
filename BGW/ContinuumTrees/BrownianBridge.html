    A continuous Brownian bridge law #

    The source probability space and Brownian motion are the actual constructions ProbabilityTheory.gaussianLimit and ProbabilityTheory.brownian supplied by the pinned BrownianMotion dependency. No existence assumption on a process is required here.

    The path is anchored as B t - B 0 - t * (B 1 - B 0). Its two endpoint values are zero for every outcome, including exceptional outcomes for which B 0 need not vanish. Its measurable pushforward to C(unitInterval, ℝ) is a probability law with centered Gaussian finite-dimensional distributions and covariance min s t - s * t.
