    Paper contour transfer for rooted measured convergence #

    Composition of the contour law convergence with the paper time convention and the divisor of Remark 5.10: convergence of the rescaled paper contour of random plane trees gives convergence of the laws of the rooted measured trees.

    Reference: J.-F. Le Gall, Random trees and applications, Probab. Surveys 2 (2005).
