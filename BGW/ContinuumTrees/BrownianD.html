    Brownian motion and bridge laws on Skorokhod space #

    The constructed Brownian motion and Brownian bridge are viewed as probability laws on the Skorokhod space through the continuous-path embedding. The bridge prefix law is compared with Brownian motion by its Gaussian density.

    References #

    • P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Sections 8 and 12.
    • D. Revuz and M. Yor, Continuous Martingales and Brownian Motion, 3rd ed., Springer, 1999, Chapter I.
