    Residual and Gaussian-tilt lemmas for Brownian bridge prefixes #

    Measure-theoretic and Gaussian-process lemmas used to compare the bridge prefix law with Brownian motion: independence of an endpoint-adjusted residual from the endpoint, densities of a pair with an endpoint weight, and the Gaussian tilt identity for the prefix density (Gaussian conditioning as in D. Revuz and M. Yor, Continuous Martingales and Brownian Motion, 3rd ed., Springer, 1999, Chapter I).

    In a Gaussian process, the residual X (u j) - l j * X e is independent of X e whenever X e has covariance l j * Var (X e) with each X (u j).

    ProbabilityTheory.gaussianPDFReal 0 (c ^ 2 * a.toNNReal) x * ((1 - a) ^ (-(1 / 2)) * Real.exp (-x ^ 2 / (2 * ↑c ^ 2 * (1 - a)))) = ProbabilityTheory.gaussianPDFReal 0 (c ^ 2 * (a * (1 - a)).toNNReal) x

    The Gaussian tilt identity: the centered Gaussian density of variance c² a, multiplied by the explicit prefix density, is the centered Gaussian density of variance c² a (1 - a).
