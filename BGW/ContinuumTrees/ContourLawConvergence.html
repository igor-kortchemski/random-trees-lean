    Transferring contour laws to rooted measured tree laws #

    The probability space and the finite tree may vary with the index. Weak convergence of the contours divided by a deterministic distance divisor, together with vanishing divisor-to-size tails, controls the discrepancy between uniform vertex mass and contour mass in probability.

    Measurability is specified for the contour and rooted-space observables. No global measurable structure is installed on finite trees or addresses. The size-ratio hypothesis uses strict tails of the real outer measure, so the proof does not require a separate measurable size map. For measurable tree-valued inputs these are the usual strict probability tails.
