    Nondegenerate increments of the constructed Brownian bridge #

    An evaluation difference has variance d * (1 - d), where d is the distance between its two unit-interval times. For 0 < d < 1 its Gaussian law has no atoms. The exclusions are essential: equal times and the pair of endpoints both give a deterministic zero difference.

    ProbabilityTheory.HasGaussianLaw (fun (f : C(↑unitInterval, ℝ)) => f s - f t) ↑law

    Differences of evaluations are Gaussian under the actual continuous bridge law.
