    Finite grids of the Brownian bridge #

    The grid has n + 1 times i / (n + 1), including zero and excluding one. Its centered cyclic rotations have the same law, as proved directly from the Gaussian finite-dimensional distributions. Distinct grid values are almost surely unequal. Thus the probability that all grid values are nonnegative is 1 / (n + 1), which proves that the continuous bridge has negative minimum.

    No assertion about uniqueness of the continuous minimum, or identification of the Vervaat image with the classical excursion law, is used here.
