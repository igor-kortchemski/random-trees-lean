    The pseudodistance coded by a continuous function #

    For a continuous real function g on the unit interval, intervalMin g s t is its minimum between s and t, and codingDist g s t = g s + g t - 2 * intervalMin g s t. We prove attainment of the minimum, its order properties, the pseudometric identities, and continuity in the two time variables. No sign or endpoint condition is needed for these results.

    These are the deterministic distance formulas of milestone C1. The metric quotient and its compactness are separate from the further proofs that it is a real tree and carries the excursion's root and mass measure.
