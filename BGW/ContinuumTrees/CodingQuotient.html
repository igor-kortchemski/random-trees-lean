    The compact metric quotient of a continuous coding function #

    Point g is a fresh copy of the time interval carrying the coding pseudometric. Its topology is the one supplied by that pseudometric; no metric or topology on unitInterval is replaced. Space g uses Mathlib's separation quotient and its canonical metric, identifying exactly the pairs of times at coding distance zero. The projection from the usual compact time interval is continuous and onto, so the quotient is compact and nonempty.

    These results hold for any continuous real coding function. They do not yet assert the real-tree property, which requires separate proofs of the four-point and midpoint properties.
