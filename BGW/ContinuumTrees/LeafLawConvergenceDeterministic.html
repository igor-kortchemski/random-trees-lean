    Deterministic leaf-measure transfer #

    Uniform vertex and leaf measures on a finite plane tree are coupled by quantile sampling. The resulting graph-distance bound scales with the metric and gives a rooted measured Gromov--Hausdorff--Prokhorov estimate. A uniform modulus on a compact set of contours makes this estimate quantitative in the divisor-to-size ratio and the leaf-index discrepancy.

    Measure change on one carrier. Two probability measures on the same compact metric space with the same root give rooted measured GHP classes at distance at most the Lévy–Prokhorov distance of the measures.
