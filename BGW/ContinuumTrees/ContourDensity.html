    Density of vertices in the contour-coded finite tree #

    Every grid visit represents the same coded point as the first visit to its address, including return visits. Every interpolated contour point is within distance one of an embedded vertex: in its interpolation cell, choose the endpoint of lower height. The two endpoint heights differ by one, and the contour stays above the lower endpoint throughout that cell.

    The zero-duration contour is constant zero, so the singleton case is included. No scaling, measure comparison, or convergence assertion is made here.
