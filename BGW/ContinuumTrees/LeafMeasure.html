    Vertex and leaf measures on coded trees #

    Uniform time is sent to empirical vertex and leaf measures by an explicit quantile map. A coupling on time pairs controls the Lévy–Prokhorov distance after projection into a coded tree. The quantile coupling gives discrete bounds, convergence under asymptotic equicontinuity, and a common-source comparison for finite plane trees. The coded-tree bound is the deterministic core of comparisons between vertex and leaf measures of large trees (J.-F. Le Gall, Random trees and applications, Probab. Surveys 2 (2005), Lemma 2.4 for the coding distance; P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Section 6, for the Prokhorov distance).
