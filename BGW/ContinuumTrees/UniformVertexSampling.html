    Uniform sampling of vertices by DFS rank #

    A time in the unit interval selects DFS rank min (floor (size * time)) (size - 1). The final time therefore selects the last vertex. All earlier rank cells are half-open intervals of length 1 / size; the final cell is closed at one and has the same volume. The pushforward of the actual interval volume is exactly the uniform measure on all vertices, including roots and leaves.
