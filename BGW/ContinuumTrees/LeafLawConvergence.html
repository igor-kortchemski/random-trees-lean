    Law-level leaf-measure transfer #

    Tight rescaled contour laws, a vanishing divisor-to-size ratio and a vanishing leaf-index discrepancy make the vertex and leaf measures asymptotically indistinguishable in rooted measured Gromov--Hausdorff--Prokhorov distance. Weak convergence of the contours therefore determines the law of the leaf-measured trees, with the same limit as for vertex measures.

    C39, law-level form (CT9). Weak convergence of the rescaled contours on varying probability spaces, vanishing divisor-to-size tails and vanishing leaf-index discrepancy in probability give convergence in law, in the rooted measured GHP space, of the rescaled trees carrying the uniform LEAF measure, to the same limit ν.map Coding.rootedMeasuredGHSpace as for the vertex measure (C38, tendsto_rooted_laws_of_contour). The measurability of the vertex-measure map (hF of C38) is not needed.
