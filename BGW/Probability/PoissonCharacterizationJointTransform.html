    Joint complex generating functions of disjoint measurable subsets of a finite-rate box.

    On each grid cell, the generating function agrees with its linear count expression when the containing cell has at most one arrival. The integrated remainder is controlled by the mean count minus the probability of a nonempty cell. Stationarity and the exponential no-arrival law make the sum of these remainders vanish as the grid is refined. Intersecting cells with the tested sets allows arbitrary box endpoints without an additional time partition.
