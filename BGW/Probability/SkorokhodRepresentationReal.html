    Real-valued Skorokhod representation by quantiles #

    The common probability space is the open unit interval with Lebesgue measure. Quantiles are characterized against the actual CDF, so their pushforward laws are the original measures. Adapted from the real quantile proof in PoissonDeclumping/Probability/Skorokhod.lean of the author's arXiv:2607.22291v1 formalization (local project, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd, Apache-2.0), to permit convergence of CDFs on a dense set, including limits with atoms. Reference: P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., 1999, proof of Theorem 6.7 (the real case by quantiles).
