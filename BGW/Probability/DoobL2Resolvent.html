    Doob L² maximal inequalities and signed resolvent moments #

    • Doob's L² maximal inequality E max_{k ≤ n} Y_k² ≤ 4 E Y_n² for nonnegative submartingales (lintegral_sq_sup_le_four_mul_of_submartingale), for real martingales in ℝ≥0∞ and real form (lintegral_sq_sup_enorm_le_four_mul_of_martingale, integral_sq_sup_le_four_mul_of_martingale), and over a compact time interval for right-continuous ℝ≥0-indexed martingales (lintegral_sq_sup_Icc_le_four_mul_of_martingale). All ℝ≥0∞ forms need no integrability.
    • For a process Z and bounded k, V with E[∫_s^∞ e^{-t} k(Z_t) dt | F_s] = e^{-s} V(Z_s) (ResolventMarkov): the martingale ∫_0^t e^{-s} k(Z_s) ds + e^{-t} V(Z_t) (martingale_discounted_resolvent) and the second-moment identity E B² = 2 E ∫_0^∞ e^{-2t} k(Z_t) V(Z_t) dt for B = ∫_0^∞ e^{-t} k(Z_t) dt (integral_sq_discountedOccupation), with the bound integral_sq_discountedOccupation_le.

    References #

    • J. L. Doob, Stochastic Processes, Wiley, 1953, Chapter VII.
    • O. Kallenberg, Foundations of Modern Probability, 2nd ed., Springer, 2002, Chapters 7 and 19.

    Compact-time Doob L², right-continuous martingale. For an ℝ≥0-indexed martingale whose paths are right-continuous (every ω), E sup_{t ≤ T} |M_t|² ≤ 4 E |M_T|²; the supremum is a countable one (rationals and T), hence measurable.
