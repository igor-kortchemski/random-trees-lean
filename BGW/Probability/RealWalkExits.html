    Measurability and fresh-start identities for real-walk exits #

    This file proves that restart exit times are measurable, determined by finite prefixes, and strictly increasing before their absorbing sentinel. It also expresses the next exit after a deterministic time in terms of the shifted walk.

    source
    theorem BGW.Probability.RealWalk.measurable_nextExit (M : ℕ) (r : ℝ) (a : ℕ) :
    Measurable fun (ω : ℕ → ℝ) => nextExit M r ω a

    nextExit M r · a is measurable (the codomain ℕ carries the discrete σ-algebra).
