    Finite cells and block thresholds #

    This file constructs the geometric thresholds and the finite families of cells used in Dudley's shared-uniform construction. Weak convergence gives strictly increasing time thresholds after which all cells in the corresponding finite family have the required one-sided mass bound. See Dudley, "Distances of probability measures and random variables" (1968), and Real Analysis and Probability (2002), Theorem 11.7.2.
