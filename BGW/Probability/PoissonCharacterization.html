    Characterization of time-simple random point measures with stationary independent increments.

    Finite-rate mark sets have finite intensity, disjoint boxes have independent Poisson counts, and natural-history compensation holds. The joint complex generating function is proved by integrating a linear remainder estimate over a uniform time grid; intersecting the cells with the boxes handles arbitrary endpoints without a separate time-refinement argument.

    The namespace is BGW.StableTrees because this characterization serves the excursion theory of the stable process.
