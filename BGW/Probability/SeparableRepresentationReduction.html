    Reduction from pseudometric spaces to their separation quotients #

    The canonical section from the separation quotient to a pseudometric space is Borel measurable. Consequently, a separable-metric representation theorem can be transported back to the original pseudometric space without changing either the laws or almost-sure convergence. See Dudley, "Distances of probability measures and random variables" (1968), and Real Analysis and Probability (2002), Theorem 11.7.2.
