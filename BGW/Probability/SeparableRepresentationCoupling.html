    Conditional couplings for the separable representation theorem #

    A countable measurable partition of S gives, for two probability measures μ and ν, a conditional coupling driven by a shared uniform variable u. Given the current point y, in the cell A i, the coupled point stays in the cell (drawn from μ conditioned on A i) when u is below the stay probability of that cell, and is otherwise drawn from the residual law. The probability of landing in different cells is bounded by the cell-mass defect cellDefect. Iterating these kernels over a sequence of partitions gives a trajectory construction over the base S × unitInterval, with the uniform variable shared across all times.

    See Dudley, "Distances of probability measures and random variables" (1968), and Real Analysis and Probability (2002), Theorem 11.7.2.
