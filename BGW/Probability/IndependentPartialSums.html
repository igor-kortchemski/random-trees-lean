    Maximal bounds for centered independent partial sums #

    Independent integrable centered increments yield a martingale of inclusive partial sums. Its second moment is the sum of increment second moments, giving Doob's explicit maximal bound. This complements the tail-based walk inequalities in RealWalkMaximal without duplicating them.
