    Dudley's representation theorem on separable spaces #

    A single uniform variable is shared by all the conditional couplings. At partition level k, a different-cell event outside one universal null set implies either that this variable exceeds 1 - 2 ^ (-k) or that the limiting random variable lies outside a fixed finite family of cells. These events are summable in k. Borel--Cantelli therefore gives small common-cell distance at all sufficiently large times, hence almost-sure convergence.

    References #

    • R. M. Dudley, "Distances of probability measures and random variables", Ann. Math. Statist. 39 (1968), 1563--1572.
    • R. M. Dudley, Real Analysis and Probability, Cambridge University Press, 2002, Theorem 11.7.2.
