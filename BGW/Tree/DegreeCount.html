    Degree-count marks in the cyclic bridge identity #

    Finitely many prescribed outdegree counts are invariant under rotating the Łukasiewicz word. The weighted cycle identity therefore restricts to these marks, without requiring positive mass or introducing another finite-tree carrier. Repeated degrees are allowed; incompatible prescribed counts simply give zero.

    def BGW.wordDegreeCount (x : List ℤ) (k : ℕ) :

    The number of steps corresponding to offspring count k. In particular, k = 0 counts steps equal to -1, hence leaves on a tree word.
