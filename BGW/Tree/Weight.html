    Degree-product weights of finite plane trees #

    For p : ℕ → ℝ≥0∞ (in applications, the point masses of an offspring law), the weight of a tree is the product of p over the outdegrees of all its vertices, each occurrence counted. Nothing is assumed about p; in particular it need not be summable to one. For the point masses of an offspring law the total weight of all finite trees is at most one (tsum_weight_le_one), and equality is not claimed here. This follows PoissonDeclumping/Bienayme/Basic.lean.
