    First-passage decomposition of forest bridge words #

    For ℓ ≥ 1, a word of length N, entries at least -1 and sum -ℓ has a first time q ≤ N at which its prefix sum equals -ℓ. Its prefix of length q is then a forest code, and the remaining suffix is a word of length N - q, entries at least -1 and sum 0. Conversely a forest code followed by such a suffix is a word of length N with sum -ℓ, and q is recovered as the length of the code (the first passage time is unique). Since the walk weight is multiplicative under concatenation, this gives, for arbitrary step masses p and every N,

    ∑ weight(forestBridgeWords ℓ N) = ∑_{q ≤ N} forestWeight p ℓ q * ∑ weight(forestBridgeWords 0 (N - q))

    (tsum_forestBridgeWords_eq_sum). The suffixes are the words of forestBridgeWords 0 (N - q), the bridges back to level 0. No criticality, positivity or lattice hypothesis is used. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Bridge/Kemperman.lean (firstPassageAt_take_unique, walkMass_neg_eq_sum); the measure statement is in BGW.LocallyFinite.FirstPassageLaw.

      First-passage decomposition of the walk weights. For 1 ≤ ℓ, every N and every p : ℕ → ℝ≥0∞, the total weight of the words of length N, entries at least -1 and sum -ℓ is the sum over the first passage time q ≤ N of forestWeight p ℓ q times the total weight of the words of length N - q, entries at least -1 and sum 0.
