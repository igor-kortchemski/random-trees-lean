    Fringe subtrees and replacement #

    Lookup uses zero-based addresses. Replacing the subtree at a valid address identifies the old and new roots; replacement at a leaf is leaf grafting. Replacement at an invalid address leaves the tree unchanged.

    t.fringe? (u ++ v) = (t.fringe? u).bind fun (s : PlaneTree) => s.fringe? v

    Taking a fringe subtree re-roots all its descendant addresses.
