    Ancestors and graph distance on finite plane trees #

    The common ancestor of two addresses is their longest common prefix. The distance is the sum of their depths minus twice that ancestor's depth. The definitions are total on addresses; prefix closure ensures that the common ancestor of present vertices is present too.

    Vertex t is only a name for the subtype of present addresses of t. Its metric is the real cast of the natural distance. No metric or topology instance is introduced on all addresses, and no separate graph representation is needed.
