    Walk weights of Łukasiewicz words #

    For step masses p : ℕ → ℝ≥0∞, the weight of an integer word x₁, …, xₙ with all xᵢ ≥ -1 is ∏ p (xᵢ + 1): the steps are k - 1 for an offspring count k. The weight of a finite plane tree is the walk weight of its increments (walkWeight_increments), and the total weight of the trees of size n is the total walk weight of the tree excursions of length n (tsum_weight_size_eq_tsum_excursions). No assumption is made on p. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Lukasiewicz/Excursion.lean (walkWeight, weight_eq_walkWeight), where p is a probability mass function and the excursions are defined through a first-passage time.

    noncomputable def BGW.walkWeight (p : ℕ → ENNReal) (xs : List ℤ) :
    ENNReal

    The product of p (x + 1) over the entries of a word of steps x ≥ -1. An entry below -1 would silently contribute p 0; such words are never used, since excursions have all entries at least -1.
