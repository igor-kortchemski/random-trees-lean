    Reconstruction from depth-first outdegrees #

    Read a natural degree word from right to left. At a vertex of degree d, the first d trees already reconstructed become its ordered children. The decoder fails if fewer than d trees are available. A tree word must leave exactly one tree; an empty word represents the empty forest only.
