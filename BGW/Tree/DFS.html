    Depth-first degree sequences #

    The degree sequence visits the root first, then each child subtree in its child order. Subtracting one in ℤ gives the Łukasiewicz increments: their sum is -1, and each strict prefix has nonnegative sum.

    This is the forward coding direction used in Section 2 of Kortchemski–Vetter, Rare subtree patterns in size-conditioned Bienaymé trees. Reconstruction and the identification of fringe segments are separate results.
