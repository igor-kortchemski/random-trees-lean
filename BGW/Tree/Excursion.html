    Finite tree excursions #

    A Łukasiewicz tree word has integer increments at least -1, total sum -1, and nonnegative sums at every strict prefix. The increment decoder checks the lower bound before converting increments to natural outdegrees.

    source
    def BGW.IsTreeExcursion (xs : List ℤ) :
    Prop

    Integer increment words of finite plane trees, with the empty prefix included and the terminal prefix excluded from the nonnegativity condition.
