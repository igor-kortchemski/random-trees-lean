    The cycle lemma for Łukasiewicz bridges #

    A bridge word of length n has entries at least -1 and sum -1. Writing S j for its prefix sums, the rotation by i < n is a tree excursion exactly when i is the first index in [0, n) at which S attains its minimum. Hence every bridge word has exactly one rotation that is a tree excursion, and (t, i) ↦ (increments t).rotate i is a bijection from the pairs of a tree of size n and an index i < n onto the bridge words of length n. Since rotating a word permutes its entries, the walk weight is rotation invariant, and n times the total weight of the trees of size n is the total walk weight of the bridge words. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Lukasiewicz/CyclicShift.lean, with the library's prefix-sum excursions.
