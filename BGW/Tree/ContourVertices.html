    The contour as a walk on vertex addresses #

    The contour starts at the root, traverses the children in order, and returns to the root after each child. Both endpoint visits are included. Projecting an address to its length gives the existing discrete height contour. Consecutive addresses are adjacent in the graph metric. The first occurrence of the vertex at DFS index i is at contour index 2 * i - depth; this refers to the address itself, not merely to its height.
