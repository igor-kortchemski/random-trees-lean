    Continuous interpolation of the discrete contour #

    The time interval is normalized by the actual traversal duration, twice the number of edges. The usual affine interpolation is expressed by the finite sum of triangular hat functions. This also defines the singleton contour as the constant zero function, with no division by zero.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.PlaneTree.contourTime (t : PlaneTree) (i : Fin t.contour.length) :
    ↑unitInterval

    The time of discrete contour visit i, divided by the traversal duration. The denominator is one for the singleton tree, whose only index is zero.

    Equations
