    The offspring generating function in [0, ∞] #

    For an offspring law μ on ℕ, offspringGF μ s = ∑ k, μ {k} * s ^ k in ℝ≥0∞, with 0 ^ 0 = 1. Only elementary facts are proved: the values at 0 and 1, monotonicity, and the uniqueness of the fixed point 1 in [0, 1] for a law of mean at most one with μ {1} ≠ 1 (eq_one_of_offspringGF_eq), and, for a law of mean greater than one (infinite mean allowed), a point s < 1 with f s ≤ s (exists_lt_one_offspringGF_le). The extinction results use monotonicity; the normalization results use the uniqueness; the supercritical classification uses the point below one. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Bienayme/Normalization.lean, where the generating function is defined on a probability mass function.

      theorem BGW.exists_lt_one_offspringGF_le (μ : MeasureTheory.Measure ℕ) [MeasureTheory.IsProbabilityMeasure μ] (hm : 1 < ∑' (k : ℕ), ↑k * μ {k}) :
      ∃ s < 1, offspringGF μ s ≤ s

      For an offspring law of mean greater than one, possibly infinite, there is s < 1 with f s ≤ s. The mean is truncated to a finite part M > 1; with x ^ N = M⁻¹ one has 1 - f x ≥ ∑_{k < N} μ {k} (1 - x ^ k) ≥ x ^ N (1 - x) M = 1 - x.
