    Compact metric probability spaces and the Gromov–Hausdorff–Prokhorov metric #

    The metric uses the sum of Hausdorff and Lévy–Prokhorov distances in a common ambient space. Its quotient identifies entire carriers by surjective measure-preserving isometries; full support is not assumed. The quotient is complete and separable, hence Polish, and forgetting the measure is nonexpansive with compact inverse images of compact GH sets. The rooted infimum adds the actual distance between embedded roots. Its zero-distance relation is whole-carrier root- and measure-preserving isometry, and it defines a genuine metric quotient with 1-Lipschitz forgetting of the root. Positive spatial scaling preserves mass and is jointly continuous, and the rooted metric is complete and separable, hence Polish. Weak convergence is stable under errors vanishing in probability, also on varying source probability spaces. Every finite iid sampled distance law is GHP-continuous, including repeated samples.
