    The excursion law #

    Let μ be an offspring probability measure and n a size. The offspring counts ω : Fin n → ℕ under Measure.pi (fun _ => μ) have a Łukasiewicz step word stepWord ω; it is a tree excursion exactly when ω lies in countExcursions n, the event {ζ(X) = n} that the walk first reaches -1 at time n. The excursion law is the law of the counts given this event.

    • pi_countExcursions: P(ζ(X) = n) = P(|T| = n), the mass of countExcursions n is the mass of the size event under the Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law;
    • the excursion law is a probability measure exactly at admissible sizes (pi_countExcursions_ne_zero_iff), and is the zero measure otherwise;
    • the atom of the excursion law at the count vector of the code of a tree t of size n is the atom of t in the size-conditioned law (cond_pi_countExcursions_singleton_code), and vanishes off countExcursions n;
    • for every set A of count vectors, the excursion mass of A is the expectation over a tree t drawn from the size-conditioned law of the indicator of A at the count vector of its code (cond_pi_countExcursions_apply_eq_tsum): the code of the conditioned tree has the excursion law.

    As for the cyclic shift in BridgeLaw, this is stated without a measure on finite trees, which carry no measurable structure here. No criticality, aperiodicity or positivity hypothesis is made; both sides vanish at inadmissible sizes and at n = 0. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Lukasiewicz/BridgeLaw.lean (map_dfsSeq_conditioned, excursionLaw, walkVecLaw_zeta_eq_bienayme). Local limit theorems are not part of this module.
