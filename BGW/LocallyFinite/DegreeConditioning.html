    Conditioning on size and finitely many degree counts #

    The conditioning event consists of finite trees with the prescribed size and degree counts. The finite-size restriction is explicit: no count of all vertices of an infinite tree is totalized to a natural number. The law, atoms and cyclic bridge mass retain all zero-event and lattice restrictions through the actual event mass.
