    Classification of the extinction probability by the mean #

    Let q = bienaymeLaw μ {T | FiniteVertices T} be the probability that the tree is finite, for an offspring probability measure μ with mean ∫⁻ k, k ∂μ in [0, ∞] (infinite mean allowed).

    • q ≤ s as soon as offspringGF μ s ≤ s (a sub-fixed point bounds the iterates from above);
    • if the mean exceeds one, there is s < 1 with offspringGF μ s ≤ s (exists_lt_one_offspringGF_le), hence q < 1 (bienaymeLaw_finiteVertices_lt_one);
    • q = 1 exactly when the mean is at most one and μ {1} ≠ 1 (bienaymeLaw_finiteVertices_eq_one_iff): for μ {1} = 1 the tree is an infinite line and q = 0.

    No source in PoissonDeclumping states the supercritical case, which the paper does not need. Convexity and derivatives are not used: the bound 1 - f x ≥ x ^ N (1 - x) M comes from the elementary inequality k x ^ N (1 - x) ≤ 1 - x ^ k for k ≤ N.
