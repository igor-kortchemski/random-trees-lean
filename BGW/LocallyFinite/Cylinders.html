    Finite offspring cylinders and their root decomposition #

    After fixing the root offspring count, a finite cylinder splits into the cylinders of the children and deterministic zero constraints at absent addresses. This is the finite-coordinate input to the uniqueness of a branching law. Adapted from Freezing1/PlaneTreeOffspringCylinders.lean.
