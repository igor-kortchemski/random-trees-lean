    Finite generations of locally finite trees #

    generation T n lists exactly the present addresses of length n, with the root at generation zero. Its cardinality is generationSize T n. The definition adapts the finite-generation construction of Freezing1/PlaneTreeGeometry.lean; indices in Finset.range are shifted by one to retain the positive child convention. No finiteness of the whole tree is assumed.
