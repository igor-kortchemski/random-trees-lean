    Geometric offspring and uniform plane trees #

    Mathlib's geometricMeasure p uses the success parameter p: its masses are p * (1 - p) ^ k when p ≠ 0. A finite tree of size n therefore has weight p ^ n * (1 - p) ^ (n - 1). For 0 < p < 1, conditioning on size gives the uniform measure on that finite size class, independently of p and of the offspring mean.

    This is the geometric/uniform specialization of the BGW roadmap. Its parameter α equals 1 - p. The endpoint p = 0 is excluded: Mathlib defines geometricMeasure 0 to be dirac 0, rather than the zero sequence suggested by the formula. At size zero both the conditioned and the uniform measures are zero; positive sizes are admissible when p is strictly between zero and one.
