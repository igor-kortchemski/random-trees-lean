    Exponential tilting preserves size-conditioned Bienaymé laws #

    If two offspring probability measures satisfy ν {k} = a * b ^ k * μ {k}, their tree laws restricted to size n differ by the constant a ^ n * b ^ (n - 1). For finite nonzero a and b, this factor cancels on conditioning. The equality holds at every size: at a null size event, including size zero, both conditioned measures are zero. No hypothesis on the offspring mean or on a lattice span is used.

    This proves Proposition prop:tilting (Kennedy) of papers/arXiv-2503.07530v2 (1)/subcritical_cauchy_final_arxiv.tex, with the two offspring laws interchanged in the displayed tilting relation. It does not construct a tilt with a prescribed mean or assert that such a tilt exists.

    (bienaymeLaw ν).restrict (sizeEvent n) = (a ^ n * b ^ (n - 1)) • (bienaymeLaw μ).restrict (sizeEvent n)
