    Canonical locally finite plane trees #

    This is the deterministic carrier used by the iid offspring construction in freezing1/Freezing1/PlaneTree.lean and PlaneTreeGeometry.lean, adapted into BGW.LocallyFinite. Unlike BGW.PlaneTree, its trees may have infinite height.

    Addresses use positive child indices, with [] for the root. Offspring counts are zero at absent addresses, but also at leaves: vertex membership is Present, not positivity of the coordinate. The finite-tree bridge explicitly shifts the existing zero-based addresses. No topology or probability law is installed here.
