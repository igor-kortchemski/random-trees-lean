    Laws of generation sizes #

    The root-conditioned child generation sizes are independent copies of the corresponding generation size. Root decomposition gives the iterated offspring generating function and the extended first moment. These marginal statements do not assert the Markov property conditional on the entire generation history. All offspring probability measures are allowed.

    Given the root degree k of positive probability, child generation sizes are iid.

    The generating function of the nth generation is the nfold iterate of the offspring generating function. This holds throughout ℝ≥0∞, including s = 0 and s = ∞.

    ∫⁻ (T : Tree), ↑(generationSize T (n + 1)) ∂bienaymeLaw μ = (∫⁻ (k : ℕ), ↑k ∂μ) * ∫⁻ (T : Tree), ↑(generationSize T n) ∂bienaymeLaw μ

    The expected next-generation size is the offspring mean times the current expectation, with extended nonnegative expectations and no moment assumption.
