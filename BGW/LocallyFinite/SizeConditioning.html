    Conditioning the Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law on the number of vertices #

    sizeEvent n is the event that the tree is finite and has exactly n present addresses. It is measurable, empty for n = 0, and equals the image of the finite plane trees of size n under the bridge. For every offspring probability measure μ (no criticality, no aperiodicity), its mass is the total weight of the plane trees of size n; the size is admissible when this mass is positive, which holds iff some tree of size n has positive weight. At an admissible size the conditioned law is a probability measure with atoms weight t / P(size = n); at an inadmissible size it is the zero measure, not a probability law. Through the Łukasiewicz coding, the mass is also the total walk weight of the tree excursions of length n, and n times it is the total walk weight of the bridge words of length n (Kemperman–Dwass in weight form, from the cycle lemma). Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Bienayme/Conditioned.lean with the constructed law in place of the source's finite-tree measure. The set of admissible sizes and limits along it are not treated here.
