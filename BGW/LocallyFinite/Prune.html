    Canonical pruning of an offspring field #

    An arbitrary field of potential offspring counts, indexed by all addresses, is turned into a canonical tree by keeping the counts at addresses that exist and setting every other coordinate to zero. This is the standard construction of a locally finite tree from i.i.d. offspring counts; it is adapted from Freezing1/PlaneTreePruning.lean.

    A retained coordinate equal to zero is a present leaf, whereas the coordinates below an absent address are all discarded: prune_val_of_present and not_present_offspring_zero together describe prune f. The map is the identity on canonical fields (prune_val) and measurable from the product σ-algebra on Offspring to the subtype σ-algebra on Tree, which is its Borel σ-algebra (see BGW/LocallyFinite/Topology.lean).
