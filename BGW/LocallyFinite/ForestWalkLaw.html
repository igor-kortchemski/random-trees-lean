    Kemperman's identity for forests #

    For an offspring probability measure μ, ℓ ≥ 1 and every n, the total weight forestWeight (fun d => μ {d}) ℓ n of the ordered forests of ℓ trees with n vertices satisfies

    n * forestWeight μ ℓ n = ℓ * Measure.pi (fun _ : Fin n => μ) {ω | ∑ i, ω i + ℓ = n},

    that is n P(|𝒢_ℓ| = n) = ℓ P(S_n = -ℓ) for ℓ independent trees and the Łukasiewicz walk of independent offspring counts (natCast_mul_forestWeight_eq_pi). No criticality, aperiodicity or positivity is assumed; both sides vanish when n < ℓ, and ℓ = 1 is the identity for trees (forestWeight_one_eq_bienaymeLaw_sizeEvent, countForestBridges_one). The multiplied form is kept: no division and no conditioning event is involved. Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Bridge/Kemperman.lean; the first-passage convolution, the conditional bridge law and local limits are not part of this module.
