    Independent groups of potential offspring coordinates #

    Group zero contains the root coordinate and the unused addresses; group i + 1 contains all addresses below child i + 1. The explicit injection branchAddress retains the coordinates needed for any finite family of child subtrees. Its pushforward under an i.i.d. field is the product of k + 1 independent i.i.d. fields. Adapted from Freezing1/OffspringFieldGroups.lean.

    source
    def BGW.LocallyFinite.branchAddress (k : ℕ) (q : Fin (k + 1) × Address) :
    Address

    Regroup the addresses: the pair (i, u) with i ≠ 0 is the address u below child i, while (0, u) is the root coordinate and the addresses u shifted past the first k children (so that it never meets another group).
