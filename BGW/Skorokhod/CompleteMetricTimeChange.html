    Logarithmic slope of time changes #

    Billingsley's logarithmic slope controls composition, inversion and displacement. The extended nonnegative range includes time changes with unbounded logarithmic slope.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.Skorokhod.TimeChange.logSlope {T : ℝ} (σ : TimeChange T) :
    ENNReal

    Billingsley's slope size ‖σ‖° = sup_{s < t} |log ((σ t - σ s) / (t - s))| of a time change, in ℝ≥0∞ (it may be infinite).
