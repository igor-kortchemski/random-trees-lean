    First minimum times for càdlàg paths #

    The lower envelope of a path and its left limit attains the infimum, including at the endpoints. Its minimizing set is compact, so it has a first element.

    References: W. Vervaat, A relation between Brownian bridge and Brownian excursion, Ann. Probab. 7 (1979); T. Duquesne, A limit theorem for the contour process of conditioned Galton–Watson trees, Ann. Probab. 31 (2003), §4; items C33–C34 of CLASSICAL_THEORY_INPUTS.md.

    source
    theorem BGW.Skorokhod.exists_min_leftLim {T : ℝ} (f : D T ℝ) (hT : 0 ≤ T) :
    ∃ (t : ↑(Set.Icc 0 T)), min (Function.leftLim (⇑f) t) (f t) = ⨅ (s : ↑(Set.Icc 0 T)), f s

    A real càdlàg path on a nonempty compact interval attains its infimum as a value or as a left limit.
