    Discrete Vervaat indices and step paths #

    Step paths include their terminal prefix sum. The discrete index is the first minimizer among the nonempty prefixes, as in Definition 6.3 of the source.

    The step path t ↦ w_{⌊k t⌋} / B of a word x of length k, where w_j is the sum of the first j letters; its value at 1 is w_k / B (C33.5).
