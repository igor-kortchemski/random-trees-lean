    Reversal of a pinned bridge path #

    The bridge reversal evaluates to the negative of the left limit at the reversed time. Unlike reversal of increments, it does not subtract the terminal value; on paths ending at zero the two operations coincide (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter II, Lemma 2, for the reversal of increments).

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.Skorokhod.reverseBridgeFun (f : D 1 ℝ) :
    ↑(Set.Icc 0 1) → ℝ

    The bridge reversal function s ↦ -f((1-s)-), defined on every path of D 1 ℝ (with f(0-) = f 0). On paths with f 1 = 0 it agrees with reverseIncrementsFun at T = 1; it preserves positive jumps (the sign flips together with the time).
