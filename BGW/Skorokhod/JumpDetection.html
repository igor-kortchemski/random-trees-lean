    Detection of jumps of càdlàg functions #

    For a càdlàg x : ℝ≥0 → ℝ, firstJumpAbove q ε x is the first time after q at which x jumps by more than ε. There are finitely many jumps larger than ε on a bounded interval, the first such time is attained, and {σ_{q,ε} < t} has a description through rational times only. Every jump time is such a first-passage time for some rational q ≥ 0 and ε > 0. This is the deterministic input to the enumeration of jumps by stopping times (P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Section 12; J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter I).
