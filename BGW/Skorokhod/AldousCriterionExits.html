    Deterministic exit partitions for Aldous's criterion #

    Successive strict exits from balls of radius ε control the oscillation by 2 * ε on each half-open block. The terminal block is included in the mesh hypothesis: separation of the genuine exits alone does not ensure an admissible partition.
