    Completeness of Billingsley's logarithmic-slope metric #

    Geometrically short matchings align a controlled Cauchy sequence. The cumulative time changes converge in the complete space of continuous maps, and their slope bounds prevent degeneration. The aligned paths converge uniformly in the complete state space.
