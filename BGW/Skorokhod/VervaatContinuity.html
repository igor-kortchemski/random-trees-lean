    Continuity of the càdlàg Vervaat map #

    A positive minimizing time and continuity there give continuity at the terminal endpoint. Positivity of the minimizing time is essential for this conclusion. Local correction of J1 time changes near a unique continuous minimum proves continuity of the transformation without disturbing the other jumps.

    References: W. Vervaat, A relation between Brownian bridge and Brownian excursion, Ann. Probab. 7 (1979); T. Duquesne, A limit theorem for the contour process of conditioned Galton–Watson trees, Ann. Probab. 31 (2003), §4; items C33–C34 of CLASSICAL_THEORY_INPUTS.md.

    theorem BGW.Skorokhod.continuousAt_vervaatD {f : D 1 ℝ} (h0 : f ⟨0, ⋯⟩ = 0) (h1 : ContinuousAt ⇑f ⟨1, ⋯⟩) (huniq : ∀ (t : ↑(Set.Icc 0 1)), min (Function.leftLim (⇑f) t) (f t) = ⨅ (s : ↑(Set.Icc 0 1)), f s → t = vervaatTime f) (hint : 0 < ↑(vervaatTime f) ∧ ↑(vervaatTime f) < 1) (hg : ContinuousAt (⇑f) (vervaatTime f)) :
    ContinuousAt vervaatD f ∧ (vervaatD f) ⟨0, ⋯⟩ = 0 ∧ ContinuousAt (⇑(vervaatD f)) (Set.projIcc 0 1 ⋯ (1 - ↑(vervaatTime f)))

    Continuity of the càdlàg Vervaat map (C34): at a path vanishing at 0, continuous at 1, whose infimum is attained (as a value or left limit) only at an interior time g where the path is continuous. Moreover V f vanishes at 0 and is continuous at 1 - g.
