    Reversal of increments on Skorokhod space #

    The reversed-increment path evaluates to f T - f ((T-u)-). Its càdlàg property uses left-continuity of the left-limit function and right-continuity of the original path. The map is the one of the duality lemma in J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter II, Lemma 2.
