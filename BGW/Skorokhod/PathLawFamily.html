    Consistent path-law families on growing horizons #

    Construct the unique law on compatible càdlàg path families from consistent laws on an increasing sequence of time horizons.

    The construction uses the Kolmogorov extension theorem of the vendored kolmogorov_extension4 (R. Degenne and P. Pfaffelhuber), applied to the projective family of restrictions; see also O. Kallenberg, Foundations of Modern Probability, Theorem 8.21.
