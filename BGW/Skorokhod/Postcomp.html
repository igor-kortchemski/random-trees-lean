    Postcomposition of càdlàg paths #

    Postcomposition by a continuous map preserves càdlàg paths. It is continuous for the J1 topology: a convergent sequence of paths is eventually spatially close to the compact closure of the range of its limit, where Heine--Cantor gives uniform control. In particular, postcomposition is Borel measurable. A spatial K-Lipschitz map gives a J1 Lipschitz bound with constant max K 1; the time-displacement part of J1 is not compressed when K < 1.
