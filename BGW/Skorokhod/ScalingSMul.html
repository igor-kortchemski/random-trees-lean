    Time and real scalar space scaling of càdlàg paths #

    This is the normed-vector-space analogue of timeSpaceScale. Positive time scaling is clipped at the terminal time, while the spatial coordinate is multiplied by a real scalar. The original ring-multiplication API is unchanged.

    source
    def BGW.Skorokhod.timeSpaceScaleSMul {T : ℝ} {E : Type u_1} [NormedAddCommGroup E] [NormedSpace ℝ E] (a : ℝ) (hT : 0 ≤ T) (p : D T E × ℝ × ↑(Set.Ioi 0)) :
    D a E

    Time–space scaling restricted to [0,a]: t ↦ k * g (min (s * t) T) for a spatial factor k (coordinatewise for E = Fin d → ℝ) and a time factor s > 0 (C6.4).
