    Compactness and tightness in Skorokhod space #

    The oscillation uses half-open partition blocks, excluding the terminal value. The modulus tends to zero for every càdlàg path, by the finite small-oscillation partition lemma.

    Uniform modulus bounds and bounded values give compact closure. The proof uses finite discretization in Billingsley's complete metric, then transfers through the identity homeomorphism. Countable error budgets give uniform tightness; the eventual form uses uniform modulus and value bounds on compact sets to include finitely many initial laws.

    References: P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., 1999, §12 (the modulus w', (12.6)–(12.7), Theorem 12.3) and Theorem 13.2.

    noncomputable def BGW.Skorokhod.cadlagModulus {T : ℝ} {E : Type u_1} [PseudoMetricSpace E] (f : D T E) (δ : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    Billingsley's càdlàg modulus w'_f(δ): the infimum, over partitions 0 = t₀ < t₁ < ⋯ < t_r = T whose steps all exceed δ, of the largest oscillation of f on the half-open blocks [t_{i-1}, t_i). (It is 0 by convention when no such partition exists, e.g. for δ ≥ T; only small δ matter.)

      Tightness criterion in D (sufficiency, uniform form of Billingsley 1999, Theorem 13.2): if, uniformly in n, the paths stay in a bounded set with high probability and the modulus w' is small with high probability, then the laws are tight. Measures of possibly non-measurable sets are outer measures, so no measurability of w' is assumed.
