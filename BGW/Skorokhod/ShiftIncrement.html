    Increment paths and the natural past #

    This file defines the path of increments after a deterministic time, proves its Borel measurability, and identifies the σ-algebra of a restricted path with the σ-algebra generated by its evaluations, the setting of the simple Markov property (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter I).

    source

    D (T - s) ℝ

    The increment path after the deterministic time s: t ↦ f (s + t) - f s on [0, T - s].
