    Aldous's tightness criterion on Skorokhod space #

    Aldous's sequence condition and compact containment imply tightness of the path laws. The proof passes through optional strict exit times, averages deterministic offsets for the short-gap estimate, bounds the number of exits, and merges a short terminal block using endpoint control. Fixed-time tightness supplies compact containment via finitely many deterministic control times and the modulus bound.

    References #

    • D. Aldous, Stopping times and tightness, Ann. Probab. 6 (1978), 335–340.
    • P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Section 16.

    Same conclusion with the sup-norm tightness (i) replaced by tightness of X^n_t at every fixed time t ∈ [0,T] (Billingsley 1999, Theorem 16.10 with fixed-time tightness). Tightness of X^n_0 alone is NOT enough: X^n_t = n·1{U ≤ t} with U uniform satisfies Aldous's condition and has tight X^n_0 = 0, but is not tight.
