    The Itô excursion measure, defined as the mean mark measure on a unit time window. Its definition is independent of the measurable modification, and lifetime slabs have finite mass.

    See Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter IV.

    The excursion measure N #

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.excursionMeasure (θ : ℝ) :
    MeasureTheory.Measure Excursion

    The excursion measure N A = ∫⁻ f, μ f ((0,1] × A) ∂P, P = stableLocalLaw θ, as the Measure.join of the law of f ↦ ((μ f) ↾ (0,1] × univ).map Prod.snd.
