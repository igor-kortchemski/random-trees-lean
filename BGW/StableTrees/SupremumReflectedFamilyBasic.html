    Reflection at the running supremum #

    The measurable reflected family, occupation continuity, contact geometry, and transfer of the stable Markov property. Deterministic restart formulas hold for every path; probabilistic statements use spectral positivity or the sealed shift laws only where needed.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters III, IV and VI.
    • A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapters 3 and 6.
