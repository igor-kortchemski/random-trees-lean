    An exponential upper bound for weak ladder renewal #

    A level cutoff is dominated by an exponential weight. The product formula for attained-height transforms reduces the renewal series to a geometric series.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteHeightApprox.weakLadderRenewal_le_exp (μ : PMF ℕ) (hc : IsCritical μ) (hne : μ 1 ≠ 1) (m : ℕ) {u : ℝ} (hu : 0 ≤ u) :
    weakLadderRenewal μ m ≤ ENNReal.ofReal (Real.exp (u * ↑m)) * (1 - weakLadderPGF μ (ENNReal.ofReal (Real.exp (-u))))⁻¹

    A free exponential parameter bounds renewal without changing a moving level to a fixed one.
