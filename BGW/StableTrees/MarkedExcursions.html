    The marked excursion point measure #

    The measure attaches the excursion cut to every positive passage-jump level. The imported modules prove its counting form, AE measurability, support, lifetime sum, finite long-excursion counts, simultaneous restart identity, fresh-measure law and independence from the stopped past, and stationarity of translated windows.

    The stopped-path enumeration proves stopped-history measurability. Consecutive windows are independent, every measurable modification satisfies the time-simple Lévy interface, and space-time scaling transports the marked measure pathwise on the full-probability support event.

    (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV, Sections 2–4; K. Itô, Poisson point processes attached to Markov processes, Proc. Sixth Berkeley Symp. Math. Statist. Probab. 3 (1972), 225–239.)
