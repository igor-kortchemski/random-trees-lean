    The Gaussian stable bridge #

    At stability index θ = 2, the spectrally positive stable density is the centered Gaussian density of variance 2. Its transition densities and bridge prefix weights therefore agree with those of √2 times standard Brownian motion. Uniqueness of the bridge with prescribed prefix laws and pinned endpoints identifies the stable bridge with √2 times the Brownian bridge.
