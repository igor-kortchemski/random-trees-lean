    Extended discounted cumulative-integral formula #

    Tonelli proves the Laplace formula before any finiteness assertion. In particular no convergence of Stieltjes measures and no recurrence assumption are needed.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.lintegral_exp_eq_lintegral_cumulative (μ : MeasureTheory.Measure ℝ) [MeasureTheory.SFinite μ] {q : ℝ} (hq : 0 < q) (s : ℝ) :
    ∫⁻ (t : ℝ) in Set.Ioi s, ENNReal.ofReal (Real.exp (-q * t)) ∂μ = ENNReal.ofReal q * ∫⁻ (u : ℝ) in Set.Ioi s, ENNReal.ofReal (Real.exp (-q * u)) * μ (Set.Ioc s u)
