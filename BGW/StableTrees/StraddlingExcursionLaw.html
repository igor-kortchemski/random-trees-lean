    Law of the excursion straddling a fixed time #

    Predictable compensation and the passage potential identify the joint law of the start, lifetime and normalized shape. The normalized shape is independent of the first two coordinates, and the start has the generalized arcsine density.

    References #

    J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.map_straddleData_eq_prod {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) {t : NNReal} (ht : 0 < t) :
    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (straddleData θ t) ↑(stableLocalLaw θ) = (straddleLaw θ t).prod ↑(unitExcursionLaw θ)

    Joint law (path part): ((g_t, d_t - g_t), normalized straddling excursion) has the product law straddleLaw ⊗ unitExcursionLaw: the normalized excursion is independent of (g_t, d_t) with the unit excursion law, and (g_t, ζ_t) has the explicit density from compensation and the lifetime density.
