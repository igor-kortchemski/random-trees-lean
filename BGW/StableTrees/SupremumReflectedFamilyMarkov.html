    Markov identities for reflection at the supremum #

    The joint law of a past-measurable state and an independent future gives the reflected transition kernel, including on finite stopped events.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters III, IV and VI.
    • A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapters 3 and 6.
