    Compact and tail bounds for weighted occupation error #

    The estimate uses compact square error and the half-rate occupation and clock means. No expectation is interchanged with a path-dependent weight measure.

    A pathwise compact-error and half-rate-tail estimate for weighted occupations.

    Reference: Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter VI.

    The weighted approximation error has a mean bound with a vanishing compact term and an exponentially small tail, uniform in the occupation cutoff.

    Reference: Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter VI.
