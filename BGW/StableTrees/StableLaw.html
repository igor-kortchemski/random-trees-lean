    The unconditional stable law and its convolution semigroup #

    This module packages the scalar stable density as a probability measure for every 1 < θ ≤ 2, identifies its Laplace and characteristic transforms, and proves the time semigroup for the corresponding laws and densities.

    References: V. M. Zolotarev, One-dimensional Stable Distributions, 1986; K. Sato, Lévy Processes and Infinitely Divisible Distributions, 1999, §14; W. Feller, An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, vol. II, ch. XVII.
