    Exponential bounds for the number of regenerated height crossings #

    The finite crossing time is the sum of its iid durations. Its Laplace transform therefore factors. Exponential Markov gives a bound for the probability of L crossings before a fixed scaled horizon.

    (heightRegenerationStop q L ω).toNat = ∑ j ∈ Finset.range L, heightRegenerationGap q j ω
