    Supremum potentials and reflected occupation resolvents #

    Analytic functions for the scale series, exponential-clock supremum tail, and occupation resolvents of the reflected stable family.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.
