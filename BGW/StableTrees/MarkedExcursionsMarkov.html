    The fresh marked measure at a passage time #

    Whole-path strong Markov transfers to arbitrary measurable modifications of the marked measure. Stopped-history measurability gives independent consecutive windows and the time-simple Lévy adapter with finite long-excursion rates.

    (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV, Sections 2–4; K. Itô, Poisson point processes attached to Markov processes, Proc. Sixth Berkeley Symp. Math. Statist. Probab. 3 (1972), 225–239.)
