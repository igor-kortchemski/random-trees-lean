    Characterization of the stable Lévy law on Skorokhod space #

    Finite-dimensional increments characterize the law and are preserved under restriction and stable scaling.

    The predicate characterizes the spectrally positive θ-stable Lévy law only for 1 < θ ≤ 2 and 0 < T; outside that range stableLaw is a junk Dirac mass and the zero-path law satisfies it.

    References: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes (1996), ch. I and VII; K. Sato, Lévy Processes and Infinitely Divisible Distributions (1999), §§11, 14.

    structure BGW.StableTrees.IsStableLevyLaw (θ T : ℝ) (P : MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure (Skorokhod.D T ℝ)) :
    Prop

    P is a law of a spectrally positive θ-stable Lévy process on [0,T], expressed through its finite-dimensional increments and its initial value.

    • start (h : 0 ≤ T) : ∀ᵐ (f : Skorokhod.D T ℝ) ∂↑P, f ⟨0, ⋯⟩ = 0

      The path starts at zero almost surely whenever the horizon is nonnegative.

    • increments (k : ℕ) (t : Fin (k + 1) → ↑(Set.Icc 0 T)) : StrictMono t → ↑(t 0) = 0 → MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (f : Skorokhod.D T ℝ) (i : Fin k) => f (t i.succ) - f (t i.castSucc)) ↑P = MeasureTheory.Measure.pi fun (i : Fin k) => ↑(stableLawAt θ (↑(t i.succ) - ↑(t i.castSucc)))

      Increments along increasing times starting at zero have independent stable laws.
