    Finite-dimensional limits of real walk step paths #

    The increments of a floor-step path are sums over disjoint blocks of the iid walk. Their exact product law at each index and scalar convergence of each block give the finite-dimensional limits.

    This is the finite-dimensional half of the functional limit theorem for walks in a stable domain of attraction (item C17 of CLASSICAL_THEORY_INPUTS.md; Skorokhod 1957, Theorem 2.7).
