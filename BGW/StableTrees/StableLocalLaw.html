    The stable process as a law on local càdlàg paths #

    This module constructs the stable process law on DLoc from its compatible integer-horizon marginals. It proves the characterization by compact restrictions and the coordinate properties used downstream: initial value, fixed-time marginals and continuity, and simultaneous spectral positivity (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters I and VIII; P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Section 16).
