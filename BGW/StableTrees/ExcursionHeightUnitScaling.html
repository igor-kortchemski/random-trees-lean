    Unit height scaling #

    Continuity reduces the all-age scaling equality to countably many rational evaluations, so the scaling events are jointly measurable. Fubini then supplies the version with a Lebesgue distributed positive scale. The raw fixed-scale identity and the unit continuous version determine a version of the scaled excursion, and the jointly measurable equality event gives the product formulation.

    theorem BGW.StableTrees.measurableSet_excursionHeight_scale (θ : ℝ) :
