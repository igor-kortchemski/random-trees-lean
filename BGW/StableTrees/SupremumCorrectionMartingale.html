    The localized zero-rate error martingale #

    Positive-rate martingales are stopped first. Simultaneous compact-state domination then passes the centered zero-rate limit through deterministic conditional expectations.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    MeasureTheory.Martingale (fun (t : NNReal) (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) => errorMartingale θ ε (errStopTime n σ t f) f) Skorokhod.DLoc.filtration ↑(stableLocalLaw θ)

    The zero-rate error stopped at an arbitrary stopping time and the localizing cutoff is a martingale. Localization of the positive-rate processes precedes the centered limit.
