    Regenerated height crossings and their iid durations #

    Crossing times are built by restarting the offspring sequence at each previous crossing. These are measurable, almost surely finite stopping times. Finite gap histories are pre-stopped events, so stopped iid restart proves independence and identifies every gap law with the first height-upcrossing law.
