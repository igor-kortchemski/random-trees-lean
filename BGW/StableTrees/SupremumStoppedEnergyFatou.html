    Nonnegative limits with uniform real-integral bounds #

    The explicit integrability conclusion prevents default-zero real integrals from standing in for moment bounds when the localization is removed.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
