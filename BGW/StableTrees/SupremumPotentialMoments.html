    Second moments of reflected occupation resolvents #

    The signed occupation identity is the generic resolvent moment theorem specialized to the reflected family.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.
