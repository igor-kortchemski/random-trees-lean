    The projective stable bridge #

    The open law has the prescribed tilted prefixes and converges to zero at its terminal time almost surely. Closing gives a measurable compact path with pinned endpoints and the same strict-prefix laws.

    References #

    • P. Fitzsimmons, J. Pitman and M. Yor, Markovian bridges: construction, Palm interpretation, and splicing, Seminar on Stochastic Processes 1992, Progr. Probab. 33, Birkhäuser, 1993, 101–134.
    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
