    Ordinary primitive formula for discounted occupation #

    The time kernel is bounded at each positive cutoff. Tonelli's cumulative formula therefore identifies the discounted occupation tail with the discounted primitive, path by path.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
