    Size-tail convergence of the walk and height pair #

    The forest pair is coupled along the full sequence and localized where the first long block ends before a deterministic horizon. Its common endpoints select both coordinates. Letting the horizon grow removes the localization. The terminal walk step is retained in the main law.
