    Public clock path properties and normalization #

    Identification transfers the auxiliary clock's continuity, recurrence and Stieltjes support. The full positive-cutoff normalization follows from convergence in mean.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
