    Discrete occupation and reversal #

    Finite-time reversal identifies the forest record count and the occupation count with weak ascending records and reflected bands, with both endpoints retained. The height scaling is B n / n, with constant exactly one.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteHeightApprox.discreteCount (ω : ℕ → ℕ) (k : ℕ) (a : ℝ) :
    ℕ

    The discrete occupation count at level a: #{j < k | ∀ l ∈ [j,k], W_j - W_l < a}, i.e. W_j - min_{[j,k]} W < a.
