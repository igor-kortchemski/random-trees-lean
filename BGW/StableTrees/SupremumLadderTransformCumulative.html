    Cumulative Tonelli formula for decreasing weights #

    Writing a decreasing weight as the tail of a measure exchanges the clock measure and its continuous cumulative function. No differentiation of the weight is required.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.lintegral_tail_eq_lintegral_cumulative (μ ν : MeasureTheory.Measure ℝ) [MeasureTheory.SFinite μ] [MeasureTheory.SFinite ν] (a : ℝ) :
    ∫⁻ (t : ℝ) in Set.Ioi a, ν (Set.Ioi t) ∂μ = ∫⁻ (s : ℝ) in Set.Ioi a, μ (Set.Ioo a s) ∂ν

    Tonelli exchanges a tail weight and the open-interval cumulative mass.

    Reference: Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter VI.
