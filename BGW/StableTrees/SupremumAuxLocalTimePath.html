    Constancy of the auxiliary clock off supremum contact #

    With no negative jumps of the original path, reflection at the supremum is lower semicontinuous. On a compact interval with no contact its minimum is positive, so every sufficiently small occupation cutoff is constant there. The same holds for its limit.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
