    def BGW.StableTrees.predictableSigma :
    MeasurableSpace (NNReal × Skorokhod.DLoc)

    The predictable σ-algebra on ℝ≥0 × DLoc of the filtration G_x = F_{T_x} ((isStoppingTime_firstPassageBelow x).measurableSpace): generated by the rectangles (a,b] × F with a ≤ b, F ∈ G_a, and {0} × F with F ∈ G_0. (Not packaged as a Mathlib Filtration, to avoid proving monotonicity/≤ m of G just for the statement.)
