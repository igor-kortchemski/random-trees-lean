    ∫⁻ (f : Skorokhod.DLoc), expNeg (∑ i : ι, c i * (ξ f) (B i)) ∂↑(stableLocalLaw θ) = expNeg (∑ i : ι, (1 - expNeg (c i)) * (lebNN.prod (excursionMeasure θ)) (B i))

    Finite disjoint measurable partitions in a lifetime-slab box have the Poisson exponential transform, including infinite coefficients.
