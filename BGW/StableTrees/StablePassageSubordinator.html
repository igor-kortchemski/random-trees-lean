    Passage subordinator and local time at the minimum #

    The strict downward passage times of the spectrally positive stable process form a càdlàg subordinator. Its right-continuous inverse is the negative running infimum, whose Stieltjes measure is carried by the zero set of the reflected path.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VII, Section 1 and Chapter IV, for the local time at the infimum and its inverse.
