    Full-filter stopped occupation convergence #

    Uniform second moments follow from the inverse-time energy and exact mean. The energy bound vanishes along the full positive filter; Markov's inequality gives maximal convergence.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
