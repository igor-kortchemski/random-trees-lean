    Conditional potential at the random reflected state #

    Convergence in mean passes the conditional occupation identity of BGW.StableTrees.SupremumPotential to the clock. Uniform resolvent convergence applies at the actual reflected state. The time s is deterministic.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    theorem BGW.StableTrees.condExp_supAuxDiscountedTail {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) {q : ℝ} (hq : 0 < q) (s : NNReal) :
    (↑(stableLocalLaw θ))[supAuxDiscountedTail θ q s | ↑Skorokhod.DLoc.filtration s] =ᵐ[↑(stableLocalLaw θ)] fun (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) => Real.exp (-q * ↑s) * supPotential θ q (supReflectedFrom 0 f s)

    Conditional potential of the reflected family: for every q > 0 and s, E[∫_{(s,∞)} e^{-qt} dL^aux_t | F_s] = e^{-qs} h_q(R^0_s).
