    Removing the inverse-clock localization #

    Eventual equality on the whole stopped interval transfers bounded martingale estimates by Fatou. The resulting maximal moment supplies an integrable domination for the means.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
