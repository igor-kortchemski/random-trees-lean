    Conditioned tree laws and degree-prefix observables #

    Tree laws use the existing locally finite carrier. Null conditioning events are totalized by a leaf, and finite DFS prefixes use the finite-tree equivalence. Complete finite trees show that every size tail has positive mass in the critical nondegenerate case.
