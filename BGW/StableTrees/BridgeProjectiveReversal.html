    Reversal of tilted bridge prefixes #

    Reversing the increments of a strict prefix preserves its tilted law, since the tilt depends only on its endpoint. This reversal is centered at the prefix endpoint.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.bridgePrefixLaw_absolutelyContinuous (θ a : ℝ) (ha : 0 ≤ a) (P : MeasureTheory.Measure (Skorokhod.D a ℝ)) :
    (bridgePrefixLaw θ a ha P).AbsolutelyContinuous P

    The bridge prefix is absolutely continuous with respect to its underlying path law.
