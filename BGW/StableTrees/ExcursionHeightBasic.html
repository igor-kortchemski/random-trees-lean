    Continuous excursion heights from almost every age #

    Versions agree with the universal height at Lebesgue-almost every interior age. Continuity and the prescribed boundary values make versions unique, even when the prefix horizon is zero.

    source
    def BGW.StableTrees.IsExcursionHeightVersion (θ : ℝ) (e : Excursion) (g : C(NNReal, ℝ)) :
    Prop

    g is an a.e.-age height version of the excursion e: it vanishes at time 0 and at and after the lifetime, and agrees with heightUniv θ e.path at Lebesgue-almost every age of (0, ζ(e)).
