    One common continuous stable height process #

    Moment bounds construct a continuous modification. Rational agreement identifies it with heightLoc, reconstructed from heightUniv. Literal prefix locality and uniqueness imply one graph event for all horizons. The compact walk/height law is its graph pushforward.
