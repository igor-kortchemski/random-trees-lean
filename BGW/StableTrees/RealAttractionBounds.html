    Normalizer bounds for real stable attraction #

    The normalizer diverges, and its ratio theorem is uniform on compact ranges of index ratios. Dyadic chaining then gives Potter bounds without assuming that the normalizer is monotone.

    References: W. Feller, An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, vol. II, ch. XVII; N. H. Bingham, C. M. Goldie and J. L. Teugels, Regular Variation (1987), §1.5 (Potter bounds).

    theorem BGW.StableTrees.IsRealStableAttracted.potter_upper {ν : MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure ℝ} {θ : ℝ} {B : ℕ → ℝ} (h : IsRealStableAttracted ν θ B) {ε' A : ℝ} (hε' : 0 < ε') (hA : 1 < A) :
    ∃ (N : ℕ), ∀ n ≥ N, ∀ m ≥ N, B m / B n ≤ A * max ((↑m / ↑n) ^ (1 / θ + ε')) ((↑m / ↑n) ^ (1 / θ - ε'))

    Potter-type upper bound without monotonicity: for every ε' > 0 and every A > 1, for all large indices m, n (both ≥ N), B m / B n ≤ A * max ((m/n)^(1/θ + ε')) ((m/n)^(1/θ - ε')).
