    The normalized prefix-density candidate at good horizons #

    Increasing endpoint cutoffs give mass equal to the stable density at zero. Bounded convergence then removes the cutoffs from every good-time cylinder test.

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
