    Strong Markov property of the reflected process for the augmented filtration #

    The usual augmentation of the canonical filtration under the stable law: the raw right-continuous filtration joined with the null sets of the law, then regularized on the right, on the completed space. Every augmented event differs from a raw event by a null set, every augmented stopping time is almost surely equal to a raw one, and every event of an augmented stopped σ-algebra differs from a raw stopped event by a null set (through the stopping time equal to τ on the event and infinite off it). With integrals under the completed law, this transfers the strong Markov property of the reflected process X − I to stopping times of the augmented filtration (condExp_reflectedFutureAt_augmented, C20).
