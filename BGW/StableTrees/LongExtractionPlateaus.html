    Exclusion of long plateaus before the selected excursion #

    A constant running infimum on a nontrivial interval belongs to a passage jump. The lifetime threshold and the least-long-label property exclude such intervals of length at least one before the first long excursion.

    source

    IsLeast (longLabelSet f) (firstLongLabel f)

    The selected label is the least long label under the good-path predicate. Reference: Billingsley (1999), Chapter 3.
