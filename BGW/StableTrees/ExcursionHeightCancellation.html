    Exact cancellation of the occupation before an excursion start #

    A valid excursion cut adds no new minimum before its start. Splitting the occupation at the start therefore gives an exact additive identity for every cutoff, including zero.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.futureInf_eq_before_cut {g d : NNReal} {f : Skorokhod.DLoc} (hcut : cutValid g d f) {u s : NNReal} (hu : u ≤ d - g) (hs : s ≤ g) :
    futureInf f (g + u) s = futureInf f g s

    Extending a horizon inside a valid cut leaves all pre-start future infima unchanged.
