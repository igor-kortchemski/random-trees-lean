    Basic survival facts for critical BGW trees #

    This module defines the survival probability and expresses it through the offspring generating function. It also records the vanishing of survival in the critical case and the growth of the stable norming sequence.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.Survival.realGF (μ : PMF ℕ) (s : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    The real offspring generating function g(s) = ∑ μ(k) s^k. It is used on [0,1], where the series converges.
