    Whole-path restart law at inverse local time #

    The everywhere stopping representative of inverse local time is finite almost surely. Strong Markov therefore gives the original law and independence of the entire shifted path.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VI.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.ae_shiftAt_supHitting_eq {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) (z : NNReal) :
    ∀ᵐ (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) ∂↑(stableLocalLaw θ), Skorokhod.DLoc.shiftAt (supHitting θ z) f = Skorokhod.DLoc.shiftAt (fun (g : Skorokhod.DLoc) => ↑(supLadderTime θ z g)) f

    The stopping representative and raw ladder time give the same centered shift almost surely.
