    Finite grids for the minimum time of the stable bridge #

    The closed stable bridge has cyclically invariant grid increments and an atomless minimum time. Cyclic invariance follows by composing full reversal with reversal of the strict prefix. These are the finite-grid inputs to the uniform minimum-time law.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VIII (Vervaat's transformation and the uniform law of the minimum time); L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
