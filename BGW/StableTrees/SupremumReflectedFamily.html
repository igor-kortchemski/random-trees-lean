    The reflected-at-supremum family #

    For a path f and a starting value x ≥ 0, supReflectedFrom x f t = max x S_t - X_t is the process reflected at its running supremum. This module collects:

    • the restart identity at deterministic and random times (supReflectedFrom_restart, supReflectedFrom_restart_shiftAt) and joint measurability (measurable_supReflectedFrom_uncurry);
    • absence of positive jumps under the stable law (ae_supReflectedFrom_le_leftLim);
    • the Markov and strong Markov properties of the reflected family under stableLocalLaw θ (condExp_supReflectedFromLoc_markov, integral_supReflectedFromLoc_strongMarkov);
    • continuity of the occupation functionals supOccupation ε and the geometry of the contact set (isClosed_supContact, exists_excursionInterval_of_supReflected_pos);
    • prefix dependence and adaptedness to the raw filtration.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters III, IV and VI.
    • A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapters 3 and 6.
