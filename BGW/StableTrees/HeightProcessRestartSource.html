    Old and fresh exploration source windows #

    A deterministic split at the restart time retains its endpoint in the old source. The fresh source is the translated Stieltjes measure of the centered shifted path. A height translation therefore pushes it to the fresh summand in concatenation. Ordered supports and fixed-time gap-freeness identify the old summand's support top.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.futureInf_shift_window (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) (t r : NNReal) {u : NNReal} (hu : u ≤ r) :
    futureInf f (t + r) (t + u) = ↑f t + futureInf (Skorokhod.DLoc.shift t f) r u

    The future-infimum window after a time shift is translated by the path value at that time.
