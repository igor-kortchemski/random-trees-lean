    Vector-path form of the joint forest convergence #

    The walk, interpolated height and padded contour are packaged as one càdlàg path with values in ℝ × ℝ × ℝ. The packaging map is continuous, so the joint product-space convergence of the forest triple implies convergence in law of the vector path by the continuous mapping theorem.
