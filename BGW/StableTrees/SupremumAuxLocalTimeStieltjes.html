    Stieltjes extension of a continuous clock #

    A monotone continuous clock on nonnegative times is extended constantly to negative times. The zero totalization is continuous as well, so its measure is always atomless.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
    source

    The Stieltjes function s ↦ m(s⁺) of a path m; junk zero unless m is monotone and continuous (same pattern as supLocalTimeStieltjes).
