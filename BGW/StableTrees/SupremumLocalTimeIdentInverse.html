    Inversion of the public supremum clock #

    The real clock discards infinite extended values. Its strict inverse therefore requires a boundary test at the common hitting and blow-up time, even though blow-up is almost surely absent.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
