    First moments of the auxiliary clock #

    Compact square-mean convergence implies fixed-time convergence in mean. The arbitrary-rate resolvent bound then passes to the clock before recurrence is established.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
