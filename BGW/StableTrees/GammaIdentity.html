    The path-only Gamma identity #

    The remaining lifetime gives the size-tail identity. Gamma weighting cancels its survival kernel and the unit prefix density supplies the normalization.

    Main results #

    • levyExcLaw_prefix_eq_withDensity_tail: the unit prefix law is the Gamma-weighted tail prefix measure.
    • lintegral_gamma_identity_path: the Gamma identity for nonnegative measurable path tests.
    • gamma_identity_path: the Gamma identity for bounded signed path tests.
    • integral_gammaA_tail_eq_one: the Gamma weight integrates to one against the tail prefix.
    • excursionTail_size_tail: the size-tail identity for the excursion tail measure.

    References #

    • T. Duquesne, A limit theorem for the contour process of conditioned Galton--Watson trees, Ann. Probab. 31 (2003), 996--1027; path-prefix absolute continuity.
    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
    • T. Duquesne, Path decompositions for real Lévy processes, Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist. 39 (2003), 339–370.
    • T. Duquesne and J.-F. Le Gall, Random Trees, Lévy Processes and Spatial Branching Processes, Astérisque 281 (2002).
    • I. Kortchemski, A simple proof of Duquesne's theorem on contour processes of conditioned Galton--Watson trees, arXiv:1109.4138, Proposition 2.3 (path part).
