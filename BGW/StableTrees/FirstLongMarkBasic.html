    Rate and null marks for first long excursions #

    The lifetime intensity has no atoms. Localizing the mean measure to bounded label windows transfers this fact simultaneously to every passage jump of a stable path.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.longRate (θ : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    The rate r_θ = 1/Γ(1-1/θ) = N(ζ > 1) = θ p₀.

    Equations
    • BGW.StableTrees.longRate θ = 1 / Real.Gamma (1 - 1 / θ)
