    Height process and exploration measures #

    Public entry point for the height approximation, deterministic reversal, measurability, erasure algebra, fixed-time occupation limits and the index-two normalization. The exploration measure has no root atom simultaneously at every horizon. Ancestral convergence, the Stieltjes bridge and ancestral support tops hold simultaneously. Exploration restarts at fixed deterministic times by erasure and fresh concatenation. The raw all-time gap-freeness claim is outside the contract.
