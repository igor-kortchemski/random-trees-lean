    Immediate positivity of the auxiliary clock #

    Scaling makes positivity probabilities independent of positive time. Continuity from above identifies their common value with the germ event. The mean potential rules out value zero, and Blumenthal's law then gives immediate positivity on one event.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
