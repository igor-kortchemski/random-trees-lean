    Reflection, restarts and excursion windows #

    Deterministic adapters for the Skorokhod reflection of a local càdlàg path: restart formulas, strict downward passage, and generalized minima on windows around excursion starts (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters IV and VII, for the reflected process and its excursions away from the infimum).
