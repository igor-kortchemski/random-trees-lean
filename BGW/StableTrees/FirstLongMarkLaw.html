    Joint law of the first long label and excursion #

    Predictable compensation identifies the joint density. The label density is exponential and the excursion factor is the normalized restriction to lifetimes greater than one.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.map_firstLongLabel_firstLongMark {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) :
    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) => (firstLongLabel f, firstLongMark f)) ↑(stableLocalLaw θ) = (firstLongLabelLaw θ).prod ↑(tailExcursionLaw θ)

    Exponential/product law: the first long label is Exp(r_θ), the first long mark has law tailExcursionLaw θ, and they are independent.
