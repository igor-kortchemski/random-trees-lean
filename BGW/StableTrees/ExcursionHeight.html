    Continuous height of a stable excursion #

    This module exports the complete a.e.-age excursion-height interface. Host a.e.-time agreement, predictable compensation of pre-start occupation and passage endpoint zeros give simultaneous locality and non-junk. Compensation transfers existence and interior positivity to the excursion measure. Surviving-age convergence identifies scaling before identification at each real age. Unit prefix densities transfer raw scaling, and the shape/lifetime product supplies the terminal trace. The common prefix graphs, unit scaling, normalization and the variance-two Brownian identification use the same continuous reconstruction throughout.
