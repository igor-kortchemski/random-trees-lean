    C27--C29 for arbitrary admissible normings #

    A real attracted norming is first converted to the integer attraction predicate. The canonical monotone norming and convergence of types provide a stable-domain norming whose ratio to the given one tends to one. Deterministic spatial scaling then transfers the sealed forest, tail, and exact-size convergence theorems.
