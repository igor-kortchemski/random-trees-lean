    Height modulus and vanishing adjacent jumps #

    The regenerated count and short-gap bounds control every short upward variation. K09 on a deterministic grid and H03 continuity control the grid endpoint values. An enclosing-grid argument bounds the full interpolated modulus. Monotonicity in the time scale gives the two-limit statement. Adjacent jumps use the modulus on the longer horizon T + 1.
