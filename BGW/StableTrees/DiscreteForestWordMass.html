    Masses of finite offspring words #

    A fixed initial word has the product of its coordinate masses. Fixed-time suffixes are independent of that word, and their walk has the scalar walk law.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteForest.seqPrefix_mass (μ : PMF ℕ) (a : List ℕ) :
    ↑(offspringSeqLaw μ) {ω : ℕ → ℕ | seqPrefix a.length ω = a} = (List.map (⇑μ) a).prod

    An initial degree-word cylinder has the product of its coordinate masses.
