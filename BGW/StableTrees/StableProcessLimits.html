    Limits of evaluations of stable-attracted paths #

    Fixed-jump times are countable. Right approximation by the other times identifies every finite-dimensional distribution of a cluster law, including terminal evaluation (Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., 1999, Section 13).
