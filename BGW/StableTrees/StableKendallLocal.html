    Local limit for walk passage times #

    Local limit for the first-hit probabilities of the stable-domain walk, used in Kendall's identity.

    References: (D. G. Kendall, Some problems in the theory of dams, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 19 (1957), 207–212; A. A. Borovkov, Stochastic Processes in Queueing Theory, Springer, 1976; J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VII, Corollary 3; local limit theorem: B. V. Gnedenko and A. N. Kolmogorov, Limit Distributions for Sums of Independent Random Variables, Addison-Wesley, 1954, §50).

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.kendallKernel (θ x s : ℝ) :
    ℝ

    The Kendall kernel s ↦ (x/s) p_s(-x), s > 0, x > 0 (junk elsewhere).
