    Stopping times and deterministic restart of weak ascending cycles #

    The first time at which the record count reaches i is a genuine stopping time with value infinity if the record does not exist; on EpochsFinite it agrees with the natural epoch. Shifting to an epoch sends that cycle to the first cycle. Prefix agreement through a completed epoch determines every preceding cycle, even if later epochs fail.
