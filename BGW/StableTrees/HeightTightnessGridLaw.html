    Probabilistic control of height increments on deterministic grids #

    K09 and portmanteau control every finite grid of discrete heights. Agreement with the continuous H03 height, uniform continuity on a compact horizon, and dominated convergence make the limiting grid-increment probabilities tend to zero.
