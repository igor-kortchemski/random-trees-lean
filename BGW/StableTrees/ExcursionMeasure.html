    The excursion measure, product intensity, Poisson box counts and stopped-past step compensation.

    The imported modules prove the Laplace functional, general and left-continuous predictable compensation, and full support identification. Support transfers through measurable compact-window lower bounds; the general predictable identity follows from finite-measure uniqueness.
