    The stable process law and the functional limit theorem #

    Tightness and finite-dimensional limits construct the stable Lévy law from the offspring-law witness of exists_stableDomain. Uniqueness then identifies every subsequential limit of an attracted real walk (Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., 1999, Theorem 13.1; Kallenberg, Foundations of Modern Probability, 2nd ed., 2002, Theorem 16.14).
