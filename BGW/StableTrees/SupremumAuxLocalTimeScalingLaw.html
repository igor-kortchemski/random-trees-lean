    Scaling law of the constructed auxiliary clock #

    The positive-filter convergence in mean identifies dilated occupations with the clock. No invariance of the particular fast cutoff sequence is asserted or needed.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
