    Unconditioned masses of surviving prefixes #

    Completing a surviving initial word is first passage of the remaining forest. The exact mass is a product, and summing over the size gives the inclusive tail.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteForest.sizeEvent_prefixCylinder_mass (μ : PMF ℕ) (hc : IsCritical μ) (hne : μ 1 ≠ 1) {n m : ℕ} {a : List ℕ} (ham : a.length = m) (hmn : m < n) (hs : IsSurvivingPrefix a) :
    (LocallyFinite.bienaymeLaw μ.toMeasure) (LocallyFinite.sizeEvent n ∩ prefixCylinder m a) = (List.map (⇑μ) a).prod * forestWeight (⇑μ) (prefixStack a) (n - m)

    The joint exact-size/prefix mass is the prefix weight times the remaining forest mass.
