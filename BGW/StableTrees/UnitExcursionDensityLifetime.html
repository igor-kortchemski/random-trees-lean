    Prefix densities at almost every lifetime #

    The remaining-lifetime identity and Kendall's density give the prefix/lifetime joint integral. Comparing it with the lifetime mixture proves the density identity for each fixed nonnegative measurable prefix test.

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.

    ∫⁻ (e : Excursion) in {e : Excursion | ↑a < ↑e.lifetime}, F (excursionRestrict a e) * g ↑e.lifetime ∂excursionMeasure θ = ∫⁻ (u : ℝ) in Set.Ioi ↑a, g u * ∫⁻ (e : Excursion) in {e : Excursion | ↑a < ↑e.lifetime}, F (excursionRestrict a e) * ENNReal.ofReal (qKernel θ (u - ↑a) (↑e.path a)) ∂excursionMeasure θ
