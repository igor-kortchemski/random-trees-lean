    Bounded stopped energy at the auxiliary inverse clock #

    The strict hitting representative agrees almost surely with the finite auxiliary ladder time. On the same event the stopped clock is bounded by its level, and the localization eventually disappears on the whole interval up to the ladder time.

    The stopped mean bound uses the centered error martingale of BGW.StableTrees.SupremumCorrection. The imported mesh lemmas and dominated convergence give the bounded-epoch energy estimate with constants 4 and 12. Nonnegativity of the centered correction allows the clock-increment term to be discarded in the grid inequality.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
