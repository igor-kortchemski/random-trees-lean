    Independence auxiliaries for the stable process #

    The increments of a stable Lévy law along strictly increasing times are independent with stable marginals. From this, the restricted past and the future increment path are independent, and the future increment path is again a stable Lévy law (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter I).

    source

    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (f : Skorokhod.D T ℝ) => f t - f r) ↑P = ↑(stableLawAt θ (↑t - ↑r))

    A single increment of a stable Lévy law has the corresponding stable law.
