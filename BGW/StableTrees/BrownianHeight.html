    The continuous height at stability index two #

    At θ = 2, Brownian path continuity upgrades the fixed-time formula H = X - I to an almost sure identity simultaneously at all times for the common continuous modification.

    Public proof helpers #

    • ae_heightLoc_two: simultaneous identification of the continuous height and reflected path.
