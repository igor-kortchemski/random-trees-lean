    Mean discounted potential of the auxiliary clock #

    The half-rate growth bound dominates cumulative occupation means. Ordinary dominated convergence and Fubini identify the mean clock potential before any recurrence argument.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
