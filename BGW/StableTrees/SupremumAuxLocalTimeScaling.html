    Scaling of the auxiliary clock #

    Deterministic scaling of the occupations and convergence in mean identify the clock under stable path dilation. The selected cutoff sequence need not be preserved by dilation.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.supRunning_scaleLoc (θ : ℝ) (c : ↑(Set.Ioi 0)) (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) (t : NNReal) :
    supRunning (scaleLoc θ c f) t = ↑c ^ (1 / θ) * supRunning f (t / (↑c).toNNReal)

    The running supremum commutes with positive stable path dilation.
