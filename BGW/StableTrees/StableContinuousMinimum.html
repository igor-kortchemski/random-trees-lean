    Continuity and interior location of the stable minimum #

    Regularity after the countably many jump stopping times transfers from the local stable law to compact horizons. Reversal of increments then excludes generalized minimizers at jump times, and regularity at zero excludes the two endpoints. Together with uniqueness of generalized minimizers, this gives a unique continuously attained interior minimum (P. W. Millar, Zero-one laws and the minimum of a Markov process, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 226 (1977), 365–391).
