    Convergence in mean of discounted occupation tails #

    Half-rate growth dominates the expected absolute cumulative errors. The ordinary cumulative formula then transfers this convergence to discounted occupation tails.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
