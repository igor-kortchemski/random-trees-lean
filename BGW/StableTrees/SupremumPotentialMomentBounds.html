    Uniform numerical occupation moment bounds #

    The rate-two occupation bound and the generic signed resolvent moment inequality give the constants required for the occupation Cauchy construction.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.integral_exp_neg_two_supOccKernel_le {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) (x : NNReal) {ε : ℝ} (hε : 0 < ε) :
    ∫ (f : Skorokhod.DLoc), ∫ (t : ℝ) in Set.Ioi 0, Real.exp (-2 * t) * |supOccKernel ε (supReflectedFrom x f t.toNNReal)| ∂↑(stableLocalLaw θ) ≤ 1

    The absolute rate-two occupation has expectation at most one.
