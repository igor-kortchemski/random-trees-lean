    Continuity of the exponential supremum tail #

    Atomlessness gives continuity by dominated convergence. The tail tends to zero at infinity and is constant on the negative half-line, hence is uniformly continuous.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.supExpTail_eq_measureReal {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) {q : ℝ} (hq : 0 < q) (x : ℝ) :
    supExpTail θ q x = (MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (p : Skorokhod.DLoc × ℝ) => supRunning p.1 p.2.toNNReal) (expClockLaw θ q)).real (Set.Ici x)

    The negative extension of the tail agrees with the actual exponential-clock probability.
