    Uniqueness of generalized minima of stable Lévy paths #

    A generalized minimizer of a càdlàg path is a time at which the value or the left limit equals the infimum. Under a stable Lévy law it is almost surely unique: two distinct minimizers are separated by rational windows whose generalized minima differ by an atom-free increment independent of both (P. W. Millar, Zero-one laws and the minimum of a Markov process, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 226 (1977), 365–391; J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VI).
