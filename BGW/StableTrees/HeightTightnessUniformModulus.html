    Uniform modulus control over all height laws #

    For each fixed index, continuity gives a small modulus in probability. Combining these bounds over finitely many initial indices with the eventual regeneration estimate gives one scale valid uniformly over every scaling index.

    The asymptotic height modulus extends uniformly to all scaling indices, including the finitely many initial path laws.
