    Two-sided regularity of zero for the stable process #

    Right-continuity identifies the positive- and negative-time regularity events with the vanishing of strict passage times. Stable scaling gives a fixed positive lower bound on each approximating passage event; continuity from above and the germ zero-one law then give almost sure regularity. The strong Markov property transfers the result to finite stopping times (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VII, Corollary 5, for the spectrally positive case).
