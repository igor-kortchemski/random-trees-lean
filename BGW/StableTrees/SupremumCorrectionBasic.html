    Zero-resolvent correction and discounted error processes #

    Definitions of the centered error and its positive-rate approximants, and elementary bounds for the correction. The auxiliary clock retains its raw adapted representative.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
