    The exponential transform on extended nonnegative reals and finite-subset excursion pgfs.

    See Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter IV.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.expNeg (x : ENNReal) :
    ENNReal

    exp (-x) on ℝ≥0∞, with exp (-∞) = 0.

    Equations
