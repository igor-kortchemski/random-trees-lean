    Mean occupation of a weak ascending cycle #

    Integer gaps strictly below a are exactly the nonnegative integers below its natural ceiling. The exact level mean and stopped restart give every cycle the mean ⌈a⌉₊, with no extra normalization constant.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteHeightApprox.clusterVisits_zero_eq_sum_levels {ω : ℕ → ℕ} (hω : EpochsFinite ω) (a : ℝ) :
    clusterVisits ω a 0 = ∑ y ∈ Finset.range ⌈a⌉₊, {u ∈ Finset.Ico 0 (weakEpoch ω 1) | reflGap ω u = ↑y}.card

    The first-cycle band count is the sum of its integer-level counts.
