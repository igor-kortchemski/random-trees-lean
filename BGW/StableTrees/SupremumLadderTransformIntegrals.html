    Measurability and Tonelli for ladder potentials #

    Inverse substitution gives measurability of the pathwise boundary integral, without a measurability assertion about the totalized random Stieltjes measure.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.aemeasurable_supBoundaryPotential {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) {q lam : ℝ} (hq : 0 ≤ q) (hlam : 0 ≤ lam) :
    AEMeasurable (supBoundaryPotential θ q lam) ↑(stableLocalLaw θ)

    The extended boundary potential is almost everywhere measurable at nonnegative rates.

    Reference: Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Chapter VI.
