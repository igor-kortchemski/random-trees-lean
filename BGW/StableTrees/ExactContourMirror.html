    Reflection of finite plane trees #

    Mirroring reverses the children at every vertex, preserving sizes and offspring weights.

    source
    def BGW.PlaneTree.mirror :
    PlaneTree → PlaneTree

    Mirror image of a plane tree: the children of every vertex in reverse order (mirror (node ts) = node (ts.map mirror).reverse).
