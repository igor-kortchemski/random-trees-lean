    Joint convergence of the forest walk and height #

    The walk marginal has the sealed CT5 step-path laws and the height marginal has the tight laws obtained by Arzelà-Ascoli, so the joint path laws are tight. Finite height observations retain the whole walk and are continuous. The interpolated K09 limits and the H03 graph identities identify every finite observation law, hence every subsequential limit. Prokhorov and uniqueness of the limit give convergence of the whole sequence.
