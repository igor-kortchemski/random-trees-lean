    Determination of a joint walk and continuous-height law #

    Keeping the whole walk and observing the height at arbitrary finite sets of times determines the joint law. Projective uniqueness identifies the associated coordinate laws; the Borel sigma algebra of continuous paths is generated by evaluations.
