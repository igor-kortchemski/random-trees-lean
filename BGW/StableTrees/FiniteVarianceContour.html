    Finite-variance contours and Brownian tree scaling #

    The stable norming gives the joint limit (√2 e, √2 e). Rescaling gives Aldous's height and contour normalization in both contour clocks. Spatial scaling of the coded Brownian tree preserves its root and probability mass.
