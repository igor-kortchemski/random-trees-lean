    Zero occupation of the reflected stable process #

    At each fixed positive time the reflection is positive almost surely: otherwise the terminal time would minimize the compact path, contrary to the regularity and reversal theorem. Fubini then gives zero Lebesgue occupation of the zero set. This avoids a separate construction of the drift coefficient of the passage subordinator (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VII).

    The zero set of the reflected process is Lebesgue-null (a.s.; measured in ℝ through the coercion, since ℝ≥0 carries no MeasureSpace instance).
