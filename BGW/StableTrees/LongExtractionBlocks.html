    Exact skip-free ladder blocks #

    Finite weak hits of integer barriers are exact. Consecutive hits delimit nonnegative blocks.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.lt_partialSum_of_lt_ladderTime {ω : ℕ → ℝ} {r k : ℕ} (hfin : walkHitTime r ω ≠ ⊤) (hk : k < ladderTime ω r) :
    -↑r < Probability.RealWalk.partialSum ω k

    Before a finite weak hit, the walk is strictly above its barrier. Reference: Billingsley (1999), Chapter 3.

    theorem BGW.StableTrees.ladderTime_mono_of_finite {ω : ℕ → ℝ} {r s : ℕ} (hrs : r ≤ s) (hs : walkHitTime s ω ≠ ⊤) :
    walkHitTime r ω ≠ ⊤ ∧ ladderTime ω r ≤ ladderTime ω s

    A finite higher hit makes every lower hit finite and orders the finite readings. Reference: Billingsley (1999), Chapter 3.
