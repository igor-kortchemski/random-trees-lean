    Independence of the supremum and drawdown at an exponential time #

    For the spectrally positive θ-stable process (1 < θ ≤ 2) and an independent exponential clock e_q (expClockLaw θ q), the supremum S_{e_q} and the drawdown S_{e_q} - X_{e_q} are independent (indepFun_supRunning_supReflected_expTime), with product and resolvent forms (lintegral_mul_supRunning_supReflected_expTime, lintegral_resolvent_mul_supRunning_supReflected). The proof splits the clock at the excursion of X - I that straddles it (compensation formula for the excursion point measure) and transfers the resulting factorization of (-I_{e_q}, X_{e_q} - I_{e_q}) by time reversal.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters IV and VI.
    • A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 6 (Wiener–Hopf factorization).

    Independence of the supremum and the drawdown at an independent exponential time. S_{e_q} and S_{e_q} - X_{e_q} are independent under P ⊗ Exp(q), for every q > 0.

    Product form in ℝ≥0∞ (no integrability needed): for Borel F G : ℝ → ℝ≥0∞, E[F(S_{e_q}) G(R_{e_q})] = E[F(S_{e_q})] · E[G(R_{e_q})].
