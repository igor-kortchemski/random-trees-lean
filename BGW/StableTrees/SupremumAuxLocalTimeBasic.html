    Auxiliary supremum local time and its Stieltjes measure #

    The clock is the raw limit supAuxLimit. Its Stieltjes extension is constant at negative times. The potential identities must be established before recurrence is used.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
