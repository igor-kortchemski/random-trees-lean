    Increasing compactly supported endpoint cutoffs #

    The cutoffs are continuous, take values in [0,1], and equal one eventually at any fixed endpoint. Their supports include both sides of zero.

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
