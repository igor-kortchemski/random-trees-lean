    Translated stopped cuts on a fixed horizon #

    The finite-horizon cut is centered at its initial point and continued constantly after its end.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.translatedStoppedCut {M : ℝ} (T : NNReal) (p : Skorokhod.D M ℝ) (g d : ↑(Set.Icc 0 M)) (hgd : g ≤ d) :
    Skorokhod.D ↑T ℝ

    Center a finite path at g, stop at d, and continue constantly to the horizon T. Reference: Billingsley (1999), Chapter 3.
