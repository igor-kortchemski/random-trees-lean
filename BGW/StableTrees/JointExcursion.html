    Joint excursion path and height laws #

    This module constructs the unit and tail laws of an excursion together with its continuous height. It proves their marginals and projectivity, identifies their prefixes using the common measurable height graph, transfers the lifetime mixture and Gamma identity, and gives the Brownian specialization.
