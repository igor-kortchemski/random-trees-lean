    Uniform convergence of occupation potentials #

    Uniform continuity of the extended supremum potential controls all initial states at once in the convolution formula.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.
