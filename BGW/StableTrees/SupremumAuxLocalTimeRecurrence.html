    Recurrence of the auxiliary clock #

    Scaling and unit-time positivity force discounted unit tests to vanish at large times. Fatou and path monotonicity yield divergence on one event, simultaneously in all time bounds.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
