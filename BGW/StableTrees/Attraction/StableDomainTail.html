    Bridge, tail branch #

    μ[j,∞) = j^{-θ} L(j) with L slowly varying gives a normalized stable domain of attraction. The step law ν = stepLaw μ has a regularly varying strict tail (TailTransfer); for θ < 2 the canonical attraction D2(i) applies, for θ = 2 either the finite variance CLT of BGW or the Gaussian criterion D2(ii) (slow variation of the truncated second moment, B3-a). The canonical norming is monotone, so IsStableAttracted.exists_stableDomain repairs its first terms. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/StableDomainTail.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
