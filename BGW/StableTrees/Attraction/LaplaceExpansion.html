    Heavy-tail Laplace expansion and canonical tail normalization #

    The tail convention throughout is the strict right tail. The canonical constant is -Gamma (1 - θ), which is positive for 1 < θ < 2.

    The proof applies Laplace--Karamata to the square-weighted absolute-step law, then integrates its transform twice using Taylor's formula and a dyadic power majorant. The negative atom and deterministic centering give negligible quadratic corrections. The final theorem supplies the one-step limit for D2. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/LaplaceExpansion.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
