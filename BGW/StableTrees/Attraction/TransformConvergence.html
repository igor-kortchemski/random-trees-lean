    Centered walk transforms and convergence of types #

    The A1 transform identities are followed by A2 convergence of types for arbitrary eventually positive normings. No monotonicity, step lower bound, or moment assumption is needed for A2. In particular all results apply with centering mean ν.

    The scale-ratio proof adapts BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.Normalization and the private proof in BGW.StableTrees.Attraction.LocalLimit: compactify the ratio as u / (1 + u), then identify subsequential limits using moving characteristic functions and the strictly negative stable modulus exponent. The two-norming version also allows a change of time with positive limiting time ratio.

    Divergence follows separately: attraction supplies a frequency with one-step CF modulus strictly below one. On a convergent bounded subsequence of scales, moving frequencies turn the normalized CF moduli into powers of that fixed modulus. These powers tend to zero, contradicting the strictly positive stable CF modulus. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/TransformConvergence.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
