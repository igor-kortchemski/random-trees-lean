    Gaussian smoothing of an attracted step law #

    The characteristic-function defect is obtained at moving frequencies. The centering phase disappears on taking moduli. The exponent of the squared stable modulus is 2 * cos (π * θ / 2) * |t| ^ θ, which is negative for nonzero t. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/GaussianSmoothing.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
