    Consequences of the Gaussian-smoothed converse #

    For infinite second moment, Fubini identifies H(s)/s as the Laplace transform of the squared-difference tail density. Karamata and monotone density recover regular variation of its integrated tail. A uniform capped-moment comparison transfers this to the original step; a second application of monotone density recovers its squared tail, or the truncated moment at the Gaussian boundary.

    The finite second moment case is obtained directly by dominated convergence, without a Tauberian theorem or a calculation of the smoothing constant.

    Main exports: rightTail_regularlyVarying, truncSecondMoment_slowlyVarying_unbounded, index_eq_two_of_integrable_sq, and smoothing_isEquivalent_variance. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/DomainCriterion.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
