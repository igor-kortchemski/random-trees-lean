    Canonical norming and Theorem 1.10(i)--(ii) #

    The domain criterion selects the tail, infinite-variance Gaussian, or finite-variance Gaussian branch. Canonical attraction and convergence of types identify the norming. Monotonicity and sequence regular variation give the slowly varying real floor extension. The earlier finite-variance helpers are retained below.

    The corrected canonical norming is equivalent to every attracting norming.
