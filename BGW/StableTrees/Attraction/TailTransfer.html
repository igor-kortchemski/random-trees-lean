    Tail transfer: from the offspring tail to the strict tail of the step law #

    Let μ be an offspring law with μ[j, ∞) = j ^ (-θ) * L j for j ≥ 1, L slowly varying. For ν = stepLaw μ (the law of K - 1) the strict tail T x = ν {z | x < z} satisfies T x = μ[⌊x⌋₊ + 2, ∞) for x ≥ 0; this module shows that T is regularly varying at infinity of index -θ (a consequence of the uniform convergence theorem) and eventually positive. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/TailTransfer.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
