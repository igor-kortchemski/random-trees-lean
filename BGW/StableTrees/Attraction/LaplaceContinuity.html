    Laplace continuity with existence for centered laws #

    If probability laws P n on ℝ have mean zero (and integrable identity) and E_{P n} exp(-λ x) → exp(λ ^ θ) for every λ > 0, then P n converges weakly to a probability law Q with E_Q exp(-λ x) = exp(λ ^ θ).

    • Tightness: the identity |x| ≤ x + 2 exp(-x) together with mean zero bounds the first absolute moment by 2 E exp(-x), which is bounded along the sequence.
    • Prokhorov (isCompact_closure_of_isTightMeasureSet) gives convergent subsequences. In any weakly convergent subsequence the Laplace transform passes to the limit: truncate exp(-s x) at height R, control the truncation error by E exp(-2 s x) / R, then use Fatou/monotone convergence in R.
    • The Laplace transform determines the law (Scalar.limitLaw_unique), so every subsequential limit is the same and the whole sequence converges.

    isStableAttracted_of_laplace_limit specializes to the centered scaled integer walk, using the Laplace identity of centeredScaledWalk. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/LaplaceContinuity.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).

    Centered Laplace continuity with existence. Let P n be probability laws on ℝ which are eventually integrable with mean zero and satisfy E_{P n} exp(-s x) → exp(s ^ θ) for every s > 0. Then P n converges weakly to a probability law Q with Laplace transform E_Q exp(-s x) = exp(s ^ θ). Finite negative exponential moments of P n (eventually) are automatic from the convergence of the Laplace transforms.

    Attraction from the Laplace limit. Let ν be a step law on ℤ with ν {z < -1} = 0 and finite mean, and b eventually positive with stepLaplace ν (λ / b n) ^ n * exp (λ n mean ν / b n) → exp (λ ^ θ) for every λ > 0 (this is the Laplace transform of centeredScaledWalk ν (mean ν) (b n) n). Then ν is attracted to the spectrally positive stable law with this centering and norming b.
