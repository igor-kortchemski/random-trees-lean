    Restricted Laplace–Stieltjes Karamata theorem #

    RegularlyVarying U ρ means eventual strict positivity and convergence of U (c*x) / U x to c^ρ at infinity, for every c > 0. The reciprocal parameterization x ↦ F (1/x) will be used for variation at zero.

    The cumulative function includes the atom at zero. All transforms are restricted to the nonnegative half line; mass on the negative half line is irrelevant.

    laplace_karamata proves both directions for 0 ≤ ρ < 1; laplace_karamata_normalization gives the exact Gamma constant. The Abelian proof uses monotonicity, doubling, and an exponential majorant. The Tauberian proof exponentially tilts and compactifies, uses polynomial approximation, and sandwiches the bounded discontinuous cutoff between continuous functions. The index-zero limit is an atom and is handled separately. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/KaramataTransforms.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
