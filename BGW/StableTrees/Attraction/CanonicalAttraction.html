    Canonical attraction #

    The Gaussian Abelian argument reuses the square-weighted absolute-step measure and the Taylor/Fubini identities from LaplaceExpansion. At Karamata index zero, its transform is asymptotic to the truncated second moment. Two integrations supply the factor 1/2; unboundedness makes the atom and centering corrections negligible. The one-step expansions then imply attraction through Laplace continuity. For finite positive variance, a general characteristic-function CLT supplies the Gaussian limit directly, without a lower support assumption. Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/CanonicalAttraction.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
