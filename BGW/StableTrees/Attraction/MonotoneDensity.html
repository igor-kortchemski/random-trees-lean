    Monotone density theorem #

    If f ≥ 0 is antitone on (0, ∞) and U(x) = ∫₀ˣ f is regularly varying at infinity of index ρ, then x f(x) / U(x) → ρ. (The index ρ ∈ [0,1) of the application is not needed.) Ported from BGWLeaves/StableAnalysis/MonotoneDensity.lean of the author's arXiv:1110.2163v4 formalization (local snapshot of 6 November 2026).
