    Potential measure of stable passage times #

    The passage potential has Laplace transform q^(-1/θ). Exponential tilting reduces its identification to uniqueness of Laplace transforms for probability measures.

    References #

    J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.straddleAgeDensity (θ s : ℝ) :
    ENNReal

    Potential density s^{1/θ-1}/Γ(1/θ) of the passage subordinator, on s > 0.
