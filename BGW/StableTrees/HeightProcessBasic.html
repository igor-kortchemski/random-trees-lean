    Height approximations and reversal #

    The height approximation uses future interval infima. Reversal is extended constantly beyond its horizon, with the left-limit convention of reverseIncrements.

    This module supplies the deterministic and measurability part of H02. The occupation-limit, ancestral, support, positive-erasure and restart theorems of the H02 contract remain open.
