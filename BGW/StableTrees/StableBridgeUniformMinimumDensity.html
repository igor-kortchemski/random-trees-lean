    Finite-dimensional increment laws of bridge prefixes #

    The increments on a strict prefix have the product stable law tilted by the density of the missing terminal increment. This supplies the finite-dimensional measure for the cyclic-invariance problem.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VIII (Vervaat's transformation and the uniform law of the minimum time); L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.

    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (f : Skorokhod.D a ℝ) (i : Fin n) => f (t i.succ) - f (t i.castSucc)) (bridgePrefixLaw θ a ⋯ ↑P) = (MeasureTheory.Measure.pi fun (i : Fin n) => ↑(stableLawAt θ (↑(t i.succ) - ↑(t i.castSucc)))).withDensity fun (x : Fin n → ℝ) => ENNReal.ofReal (stableDensityAt θ (1 - a) (-∑ i : Fin n, x i) / Scalar.stableDensity θ 0)
