    Pre-return duality and finiteness of weak ascending epochs #

    Negative prefixes are expressed without a totalized hitting time, and finite reversal identifies their level occupations with first downward passages. Duality thus computes occupation before the first return without assuming its finiteness. Factoring the final independent step and summing offspring tails gives return mass one, since the offspring mean is one. Shift invariance then gives all weak epochs.
