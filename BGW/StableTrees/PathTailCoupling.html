    Full-sequence coupling for localized long-block extraction #

    The compact-horizon FCLT is represented along the entire sequence. Graph disintegration lifts the limit to the local stable law. Reconstructed mesh increments then satisfy K04 on the K05 good event, with no subsequence change of the mesh index.
