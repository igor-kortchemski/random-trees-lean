    The uniform minimum time of the stable bridge #

    The proof uses cyclically exchangeable finite grids and convergence of their minimum times.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VIII (Vervaat's transformation and the uniform law of the minimum time); L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.stableBridgeLaw_vervaatTime_le {θ x : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) (hx : x ∈ Set.Icc 0 1) :
    ↑(stableBridgeLaw θ) {f : Skorokhod.D 1 ℝ | ↑(Skorokhod.vervaatTime f) ≤ x} = ENNReal.ofReal x

    The minimum time of the stable bridge is uniform on [0,1].
