    One-sided rise control from discrete regeneration #

    Integer forest heights increase by at most one in a step. A first crossing therefore reaches its level without overshooting. The shifted-height identity bounds every upward variation within a regenerated gap. A bounded number of crossings and long gaps then give simultaneous one-sided control at all integer times in the horizon, with an exceptional event of arbitrarily small probability on a sufficiently short time scale, uniformly for all large scaling indices.
