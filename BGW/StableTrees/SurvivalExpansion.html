    The offspring generating-function expansion at the stable scale #

    The stable Laplace asymptotic is transferred from the canonical norming to any attracting norming, and then from the exponential generating function to its real argument 1 - λ / Bₙ.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.Survival.centeredLaplace_realGF_difference_bound (μ : PMF ℕ) (hc : IsCritical μ) {u : ℝ} (hu0 : 0 ≤ u) (hu1 : u ≤ 1) :
    |Attraction.LaplaceExpansion.centeredLaplace (stepLaw μ) u - 1 - (realGF μ (1 - u) - (1 - u))| ≤ 4 * u ^ 2
