    Tail-conditioned tree walk paths #

    The joint endpoints and cut of the first long block converge by a full-sequence Skorokhod representation localized at a deterministic source horizon. K02 coding and the uniform closing comparison identify the tail-conditioned tree-path limit on every positive horizon.

    |(treePrefixPath B n T τ) t - (Skorokhod.realStepPath (↑T) n (B n) (closedTreeIncrementSeq τ)) t| ≤ |1 / B n|
