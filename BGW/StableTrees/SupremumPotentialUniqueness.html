    Laplace uniqueness for finite measures on the nonnegative half-line #

    Two finite measures carried by [0, ∞) are equal when their Laplace transforms agree at all positive parameters. The proof uses analyticity in the left half-plane and dominated convergence at its imaginary boundary (W. Feller, An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, Vol. II, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1971, Section XIII.1).
