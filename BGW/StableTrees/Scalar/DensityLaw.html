    d_α is the density of Y_α (Stage 1, completed) #

    Source line 864 ("d_α is the density of Y_α"). The function BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.stableDensity α is defined by Fourier inversion of exp((-iθ)^α); here we prove that it is indeed the density of the limit law:

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.stableDensity_nonneg: d_α ≥ 0 (from the local limit theorem);
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.integral_stableDensity_mul_gaussian_le: ∫ d_α(x) e^{-bx²} dx ≤ 1 (Fubini and the Gaussian Fourier transform);
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.integrable_stableDensity: d_α ∈ L¹ (monotone convergence);
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.charFun_withDensity_stableDensity: the characteristic function of d_α(x) dx is exp((-iθ)^α) (mathlib's Fourier inversion theorem);
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.limitLaw_eq_withDensity: ν = d_α(x) dx;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.integral_stableDensity: ∫ d_α = 1.

    The hypothesis StableDomain μ α a is used only through d_α ≥ 0 (a property of α alone, obtained here from the local limit theorem of any walk in the domain).
