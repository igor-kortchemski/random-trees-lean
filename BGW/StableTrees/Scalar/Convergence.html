    Characteristic-function convergence of the normalized walk #

    Under (H_α) (BGW.StableTrees.ScalarDomain μ α a), the laws of S_m / a_m (BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.scaledLaw) converge weakly to ν, whose characteristic function is exp((-iθ)^α) (BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.charFun_eq_stableCF).

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.tendsto_stepCF_pow: for times ψ j → ∞ and arguments s_j → t, φ(s_j / a_{ψ j})^{ψ j} → exp((-it)^α); the moving argument is handled by the uniform modulus |E e^{isY} - E e^{itY}| ≤ E min(2, |s-t||Y|) and the weak convergence of the bounded continuous test function min(2, δ|x|) (no tightness argument is needed).
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.tendsto_norm_stepCF_pow: the same for |φ|, with limit exp(cos(πα/2)|t|^α).

    Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Stable/Convergence.lean in igor-kortchemski/rare-subtree-patterns, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd (Apache-2.0).
