    Local and bridge-weighted tail bounds for finite forest sizes #

    The total forest weight is proved from the existing finite-tree weights and the Bienaymé law. For every offspring PMF it is at most one. The local and tail bounds then follow from StableDomain, the scalar local limit theorem, Kemperman's identity, and regular variation. No extra estimate package is assumed, and positivity of the normalizer is used only at positive times. The lower size bound and the normalized bridge bound retain explicit admissibility; periodic offspring laws are allowed.

    Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Bienayme/Basic.lean and PoissonDeclumping/Bridge/ForestEstimates.lean in igor-kortchemski/rare-subtree-patterns, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd (Apache-2.0). The source uses tuples of its own finite trees; here all sums use the existing BGW.forestWeight on lists of BGW.PlaneTree. The lower endpoint bound adapts the finite-minimum argument of PoissonDeclumping/Setup/Toolkit.lean directly to the proved lattice local limit theorem, without a toolkit hypothesis.
