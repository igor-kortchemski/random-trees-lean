    Tightness of the actual maximum of the walk's partial sums #

    The maximum here is evaluated on a single product-law walk path, rather than a maximum of marginal probabilities. An Ottaviani first-exit decomposition and fresh-increment independence upgrade the scalar tightness derived from (H_α) to maximal tightness. This is a consequence of the functional convergence asserted at source lines 846–850, not a proof of that full Skorokhod-topology convergence.
