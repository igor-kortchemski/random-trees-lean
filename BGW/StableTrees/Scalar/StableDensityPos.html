    Strict positivity of the stable density #

    The convolution semigroup propagates positivity of p₁(y) to pₙ(n y) for every positive integer n. Scaling then propagates positivity to every real argument: n^(1/θ - 1) x tends to zero, where the density is continuous and strictly positive. This also proves strict positivity of the Gamma factor in Kendall's identity.

    Reference: V. M. Zolotarev, One-dimensional Stable Distributions, AMS, 1986, Section 2.7.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.stableDensity_pos {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) (x : ℝ) :
    0 < Scalar.stableDensity θ x

    The stable density is strictly positive on all of ℝ.
