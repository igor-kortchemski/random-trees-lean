    The scalar walk on one product probability space #

    The product law of the increments is the coordinatewise image of independent offspring counts. Its sum has the convolution law. Prefix and suffix events are independent, with all split-time bounds explicit.

    Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Cyclic/WalkBridge.lean, PoissonDeclumping/Bridge/WalkCalculus.lean in igor-kortchemski/rare-subtree-patterns, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd (Apache-2.0).
