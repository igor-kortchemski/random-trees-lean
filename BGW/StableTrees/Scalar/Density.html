    The stable density d_α (Stage 1, continued) #

    Source lines 862–864 and the uses of d_α at lines 1029–1041, 1640–1647, 1987–1994. The density of Y_α is defined by Fourier inversion of the (integrable) characteristic function exp((-iθ)^α) from BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.LimitLaw:

    d_α(x) = (1/2π) Re ∫ exp((-iθ)^α) e^{-iθx} dθ.

    We prove that d_α is bounded and Lipschitz (hence continuous and uniformly continuous), and compute its value at zero, d_α(0) = Γ(1 + 1/α) sin(π/α) / π > 0, using the complex Gamma integral of BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.Analytic (this replaces the contour rotation).
