    Weighted bounds for the stable density #

    Differentiation under Fourier inversion and integration by parts give boundedness of the density and its derivative after multiplication by the spatial variable.

    Reference: V. M. Zolotarev, One-dimensional Stable Distributions, AMS, 1986, Section 2.4.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.exists_abs_mul_stableDensity_le {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) :
    ∃ (C : ℝ), ∀ (x : ℝ), |x * Scalar.stableDensity θ x| ≤ C

    sup_x |x p_1(x)| < ∞.
