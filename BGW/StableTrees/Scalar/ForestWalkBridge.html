    The scalar convolution law in Kemperman's forest identity #

    The existing forest weights and offspring-count product law agree with the scalar convolution walk. The multiplied identity holds for ℓ ≥ 1 and every time n; division by n is restricted to positive times. No criticality or lattice assumption is needed.

    The scalar form adapts PoissonDeclumping/Bridge/Kemperman.lean in igor-kortchemski/rare-subtree-patterns, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd (Apache-2.0), using the forest identity already proved in BGW.LocallyFinite.ForestWalkLaw.
