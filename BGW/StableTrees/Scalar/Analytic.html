    Analytic helpers for the stable toolbox #

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.eqOn_halfPlane_of_eq_real: identity theorem on a left half-plane {Re z < c} from agreement at real points.
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.integral_cexp_neg_mul_rpow: the complex Gamma integral ∫_0^∞ exp(-r θ^p) dθ = Γ(1/p + 1) r^{-1/p} for Re r > 0 (mathlib only has real r); proved by analytic continuation in r, through the complex moment generating function of θ ↦ θ^p under the finite measure e^{-θ^p} dθ on (0,∞).
    • integrability on ℝ of |θ|^s exp(-c|θ|^p).

    These are used to identify the characteristic function and the density of the limit law (source lines 846–864).
