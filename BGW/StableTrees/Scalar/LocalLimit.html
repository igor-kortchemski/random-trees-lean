    The (lattice) local limit theorem (Stage 4) #

    Source lines 855–883 (inventory B09, eq:LL and its lattice version; the paper cites Ibragimov–Linnik, Theorem 4.2.1). Under (H_α) alone, with d = latticeSpan μ the span of μ (periodic laws allowed) and d_α = stableDensity α:

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.localLimit: uniformly over k ∈ -n + dℤ, |a_n P(S_n = k) - d · d_α(k / a_n)| → 0;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.walkProb_eq_zero_of_not_dvd: P(S_n = k) = 0 off -n + dℤ;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.localLimit_aperiodic: the aperiodic statement eq:LL;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.localLimit_of_support: the paper's form with the lattice nb + dℤ for an arbitrary support point b of X_1.

    Proof: Fourier inversion on ℤ reduced to [-π/d, π/d] by the twisted periodicity of φ, rescaling θ = t/a_n, and the L¹ convergence ∫ |1_{|t| ≤ π a_n/d} φ(t/a_n)^n - exp((-it)^α)| dt → 0 (dominated convergence on |t| ≤ δ a_n with the Stage 3 bound, and a_n q^n → 0 on δ a_n ≤ |t| ≤ π a_n/d).
