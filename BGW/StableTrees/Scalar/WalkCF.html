    Characteristic function of the walk and Fourier inversion on ℤ #

    For the step law X = K - 1 (BGW.StableTrees.stepLaw) and the walk law BGW.StableTrees.walkLaw μ n (an n-fold convolution, source lines 174–181, 842–845):

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.walkCF_eq_pow: E e^{iθ S_n} = φ(θ)^n with φ = stepCF μ;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.charFun_walkLaw_map_div: the characteristic function of S_n / c is φ(t/c)^n;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.walkLaw_real_singleton: Fourier inversion on ℤ, P(S_n = k) = (1/2π) ∫_{-π}^{π} φ(θ)^n e^{-ikθ} dθ.
