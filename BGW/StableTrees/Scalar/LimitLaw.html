    The limit law Y_α: characteristic function and uniqueness (Stage 1) #

    Source lines 174–178 and 846: Y_α has Laplace transform E exp(-λ Y_α) = exp(λ^α) for λ > 0, with α ∈ (1,2]. We prove:

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.charFun_eq_stableCF: E exp(iθY_α) = exp((-iθ)^α) (principal complex power), by analytic continuation of the Laplace transform to {Re z < 0} (identity theorem for the complex moment generating function) and dominated convergence to the boundary Re z = 0;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.norm_stableCF: |E exp(iθY_α)| = exp(cos(πα/2) |θ|^α), where cos(πα/2) < 0 for α ∈ (1,2] (cos(πα/2) = -1 for α = 2);
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.integrable_stableCF: the characteristic function is integrable;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.limitLaw_unique: the law with this Laplace transform is unique.
