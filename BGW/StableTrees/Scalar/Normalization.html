    Regular variation of the normalizing sequence (Stage 2) #

    Source lines 846–853 (inventory B06): under (H_α) the normalizing sequence (a_n) is regularly varying of index 1/α, i.e. a_n / n^{1/α} is slowly varying. The paper cites Feller; here it is derived from the convergence S_n / a_n → Y_α by a convergence-of-types argument on the moduli of characteristic functions:

    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.tendsto_div_of_tendsto_div: if m'_n / n → κ > 0 then a_n / a_{m'_n} → κ^{-1/α} (Bolzano–Weierstrass on u/(1+u), identification of the limit through |φ(1/a_{m'})|^{m'} = (|φ(u/a_n)|^n)^{m'/n} and |E e^{itY_α}| = exp(cos(πα/2)|t|^α));
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.regularlyVaryingSeq: RegularlyVaryingSeq (1/α) a;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.slowlyVarying: x ↦ a_{⌊x⌋} / x^{1/α} is slowly varying;
    • BGW.StableTrees.Scalar.tendsto_atTop: a_n → ∞.

    No truncated-second-moment criterion is assumed; the proof starts from normalized weak convergence.

    Adapted from PoissonDeclumping/Stable/Normalization.lean in igor-kortchemski/rare-subtree-patterns, commit fc5ef722392b1af41e8b9d5fc45d5a521b7823dd (Apache-2.0).
