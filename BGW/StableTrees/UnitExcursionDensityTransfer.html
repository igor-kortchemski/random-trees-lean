    Transferring absence of fixed jumps through prefix densities #

    Restriction preserves the left limit at each positive prefix time. Absolute continuity of the weighted excursion measure transfers the fixed-time property.

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
