    Excluding predictable null events at every jump atom #

    The predictable bad slope event is null at deterministic levels: the full-filter finite-reading estimate gives the dyadic extended-reading estimate at each fixed positive level, all conversions taking place on the common finite-passage event.

    General predictable compensation on finite level and lifetime boxes then excludes a predictable event that is null at each deterministic positive level. Countable exhaustion retains every excursion atom on one host event.
