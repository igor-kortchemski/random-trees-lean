    Regularity of the stable scale series #

    Compact majorants justify continuity and termwise differentiation.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.

    ContinuousOn (stableSeries θ q) (Set.Ici 0)
