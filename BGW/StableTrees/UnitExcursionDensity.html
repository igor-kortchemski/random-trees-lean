    Unit excursion prefix density and absence of fixed jumps #

    The prefix law at every interior horizon is the normalized Kendall-weighted excursion survival measure. In particular the unit law has no fixed jumps.

    Main results #

    • unitPrefix: the law of the prefix of the unit excursion up to an interior time.
    • levyExcLaw_prefix_density: unitPrefix θ a is the normalized Kendall-weighted excursion survival measure, pushed forward by the restriction to [0, a].
    • lintegral_unitPrefix_eq: integral form of the prefix density for nonnegative measurable tests.
    • levyExcLaw_noFixedJump: the unit law has no fixed jump at an interior time.
    • exists_goodTimes_unit: a countable dense set of times without fixed jumps.
    • prefix_density_ae_lifetime: the prefix density identity at almost every lifetime for a fixed nonnegative measurable test.

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.

    • T. Duquesne, Path decompositions for real Lévy processes, Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist. 39 (2003), 339–370.
