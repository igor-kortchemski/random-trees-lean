    Integrable discounted cumulative clocks #

    The half-rate first-moment bound gives product integrability. This precedes and justifies conversion from extended integrals to the actual Stieltjes real integrals.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
