    Marchal law along its unique histories #

    The unique-parent and injective-position lemmas reduce the mass of a graft to a single term in the transition and in the bind defining the chain.

    Every labelled state has a graft decomposition. Induction therefore gives the all-state product formula, and the binary specialization gives the exact count of binary states by normalization of the probability mass function.

    Each state has a unique history (remove the last leaf and suppress the resulting degree-two vertex), so its probability is a single product of selection weights. References: P. Marchal, A note on the fragmentation of a stable tree, DMTCS Proc. (2008); N. Curien and B. Haas, The stable trees are nested, arXiv:1207.5418, §§1.1, 1.3.
