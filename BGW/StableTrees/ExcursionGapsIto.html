    Itô’s excursion family indexed by lifetime #

    The scaled unit laws have exact lifetimes and satisfy the stable scaling group law. Restricting the lifetime mixtures to measurable lifetime sets identifies a competing family almost everywhere; a countable generating algebra removes the exceptional lifetime, and scaling proves uniqueness at every positive lifetime.
