    Support of ancestral exploration measures #

    Future-infimum Stieltjes measures agree strictly before an ancestor. Clock order separates the earlier and later heights, transporting the fixed-horizon support bounds to every ancestor. Rational horizons give one event for ancestral support tops. The final window identity records the deterministic source truncation needed for exploration restart.
