    Extended occupation limit for the germ argument #

    The ENNReal occupation limsup is monotone on every path and prefix measurable. This local helper uses only supAuxLimit, without any identification of the public local time. Its germ zero-one law does not by itself exclude the zero clock: the mean potential must first be established independently of recurrence.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
