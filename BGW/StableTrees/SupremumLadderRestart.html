    Restart at inverse local time and the ascending ladder pair #

    Occupation additivity at a contact time identifies the restarted public clock. Strict inversion then identifies the ladder increments with the ladder of the independent future.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VI (ladder processes).
    • A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 6.
