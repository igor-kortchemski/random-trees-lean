    Restart and independence of weak ascending cycles #

    History events are saturated under agreement up to the genuine extended epoch. They are pre-stopped events on every sequence, while agreeing almost surely with the finite-cycle history. This keeps totalized epochs out of the stopping-time premise.

    ↑(DiscreteForest.offspringSeqLaw μ) ({ω : ℕ → ℕ | ∀ j ∈ S, clusterVisits ω a j ∈ A j} ∩ {ω : ℕ → ℕ | clusterVisits ω a i ∈ C}) = ↑(DiscreteForest.offspringSeqLaw μ) {ω : ℕ → ℕ | ∀ j ∈ S, clusterVisits ω a j ∈ A j} * ↑(DiscreteForest.offspringSeqLaw μ) {ω : ℕ → ℕ | clusterVisits ω a 0 ∈ C}
