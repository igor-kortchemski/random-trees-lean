    Identification of the joint height limit #

    The exact finite-path occupation map converges by continuous mapping, and its distance from the discrete occupation vector is bounded by the final cell length. A joint vector error is covered by finitely many coordinate errors, and upper limits preserve the finite-sum bound. Choose positive bands tending to zero outside the exceptional countable set. The discrete two-limit estimate and the sealed continuum approximation identify both sides of the fixed-band joint convergence.
