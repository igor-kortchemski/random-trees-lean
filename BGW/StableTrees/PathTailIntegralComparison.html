    Comparing test integrals through localized reconstruction #

    The comparison uses equality on a finite-prefix event and full-sequence convergence on a large good event. All error terms are bounded by measurable indicators.

    Scalar integral comparison when reconstruction is almost everywhere exact on observable events and converges on a large good event. The estimate pays four times the test bound on the bad limiting event.
