    Stable bridge prefix laws #

    The stable transition density defines normalized, consistent changes of measure on every strict prefix of the unit interval.

    References: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes (1996), ch. VIII (bridges); L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997); item C31 of CLASSICAL_THEORY_INPUTS.md.
