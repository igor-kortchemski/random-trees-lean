    Public local time at the supremum and ascending ladder processes #

    For the spectrally positive θ-stable process (1 < θ ≤ 2), this entry point collects the local time at the running supremum, constructed as the almost sure limit of the occupation approximations ε⁻¹ Leb{s ≤ t : S_s − X_s < ε} along a deterministic sequence, its inverse, the ascending ladder time and height processes, their exact Wiener–Hopf transform E exp(−q A_z − λ B_z) = exp(−z (q − λ^θ)/(q^{1/θ} − λ)), the restart identity at ladder times and stopped L² estimates. All modules below are in BGW.StableTrees and are reexported here.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapters IV, VI and VII.
    • T. Duquesne and J.-F. Le Gall, Random Trees, Lévy Processes and Spatial Branching Processes, Astérisque 281 (2002), Section 1.1.
