    Convergence of nonnegative weights with mass one #

    Scheffé's argument uses dominated convergence for the minimum of the two weights. Convergence in measure is reduced to almost everywhere convergence on subsequences.

    Reference: J.-F. Le Gall, Random trees and applications, Probab. Surveys 2 (2005), Section 1.

    Filter.Tendsto (fun (k : ℕ) => ∫ (ω : Ω), |w k ω - w₀ ω| ∂P) Filter.atTop (nhds 0)

    Scheffé for nonnegative mass-one weights (generic helper). If w_k ≥ 0, w ≥ 0, all with integral one and w_k → w in measure, then w_k → w in L¹.

    Filter.Tendsto (fun (k : ℕ) => ∫ (ω : Ω), F k ω * w k ω ∂P) Filter.atTop (nhds (∫ (ω : Ω), F₀ ω * w₀ ω ∂P))

    Weighted convergence (consequence): uniformly bounded measurable F_k → F in measure and weights as above give ∫ F_k w_k → ∫ F w₀.
