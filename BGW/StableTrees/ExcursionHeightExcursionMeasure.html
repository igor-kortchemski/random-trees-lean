    Continuous height versions under the excursion measure #

    The measurable non-junk event transfers from every jump mark to the excursion measure. The deterministic prefix graph then holds simultaneously at all surviving horizons.

    ∀ᵐ (e : Excursion) ∂excursionMeasure θ, ∃ (g : C(NNReal, ℝ)), IsExcursionHeightVersion θ e g

    Under the excursion measure every excursion has an a.e.-age height version (the non-junk branch of excursionHeight).
