    Tightness of real walk step paths #

    The laws of the floor-step paths of a real iid walk are tight on Skorokhod space. The proof transfers compact containment and modulus bounds from the walk to its path law.

    This is the tightness half of the functional limit theorem for walks in a stable domain of attraction (item C17 of CLASSICAL_THEORY_INPUTS.md), through Billingsley's criterion with the modulus w' (P. Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 1999, Theorem 13.2).

    MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure (Skorokhod.D T ℝ)

    The law of the floor-step path t ↦ S_{⌊n t⌋} / B n on [0,T], as the genuine pushforward of iidLaw ν under the canonical walk map. The normalizer B n is arbitrary; no attraction hypothesis is needed to define the law.
