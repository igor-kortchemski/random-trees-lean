    Dense good times for the unit excursion law #

    Fixed jumps of a probability law on càdlàg paths form a countable set. Removing them yields a countable set meeting every open subinterval of (0,1).

    References #

    • L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
