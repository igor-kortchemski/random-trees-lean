    The minimum of the stable bridge and continuity of the Vervaat transform #

    Strict prefixes of the closed stable bridge are absolutely continuous with respect to the stable process. The unique continuous interior minimum theorem therefore applies to every dyadic prefix. A countable intersection of these events gives uniqueness, interiority and continuity of the global generalized minimum of the bridge. The deterministic Vervaat continuity theorem then applies, and strict positivity away from the endpoints follows from uniqueness of the minimizing time (L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403; W. Vervaat, A relation between Brownian bridge and Brownian excursion, Ann. Probab. 7 (1979), 143–149).
