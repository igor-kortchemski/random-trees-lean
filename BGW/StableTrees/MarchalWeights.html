    Marchal selection weights on a finite labelled tree #

    The graph is an existing finite SimpleGraph, with its leaves enumerated injectively by Fin (n + 1). Positions are edges or vertices of degree at least three. No plane order, tree-state quotient, or growth operation is introduced here.

    The intended selection weights are those of Curien–Haas, The stable trees are nested, arXiv:1207.5418v1, Section 1.3, equation (2): an edge has weight α - 1, and a vertex of degree d ≥ 3 has weight d - 1 - α.
