    Measurability of the marked excursion measure #

    Rational physical times enumerate all passage-jump labels through local time. Keeping the first occurrence of each label yields a measurable sum of Dirac masses.

    (J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV, Sections 2–4; K. Itô, Poisson point processes attached to Markov processes, Proc. Sixth Berkeley Symp. Math. Statist. Probab. 3 (1972), 225–239.)
