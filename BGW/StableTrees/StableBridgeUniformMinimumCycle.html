    A finite cyclic-invariance criterion #

    On the zero-sum hyperplane, reversal of all coordinates and reversal of the initial coordinates generate cyclic shifts.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter VIII (Vervaat's transformation and the uniform law of the minimum time); L. Chaumont, Excursion normalisée, méandre et pont pour les processus de Lévy stables, Bull. Sci. Math. 121 (1997), 377–403.
