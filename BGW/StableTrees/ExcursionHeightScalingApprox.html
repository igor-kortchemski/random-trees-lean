    Exact scaling of excursion height approximations #

    Positive space-time dilation transports interval infima and multiplies occupation time by the time dilation. No sequence identification or probability law enters this calculation.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.futureInf_scaleLoc (θ : ℝ) (c : ↑(Set.Ioi 0)) (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) {s t : NNReal} (hst : s ≤ t) :
    futureInf (scaleLoc θ c f) ((↑c).toNNReal * t) ((↑c).toNNReal * s) = ↑c ^ (1 / θ) * futureInf f t s

    Positive stable scaling transports a future infimum on the corresponding time window.
