    Killed stable paths and excursion futures #

    The killed stable kernel uses strict downward passage of the driving process. The excursion future is uncentred, and its lifetime is read by the last positive time.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
