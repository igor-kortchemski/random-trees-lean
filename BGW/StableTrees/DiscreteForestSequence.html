    Laws of shifted offspring sequences #

    Coordinate shifts preserve the iid offspring law, and the real increments have the canonical iid real-walk law. Stopping times of the coordinate filtration and their pre-stopped events are also defined here.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteForest.map_shiftSeq_offspringSeqLaw (μ : PMF ℕ) (m : ℕ) :
    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (shiftSeq m) ↑(offspringSeqLaw μ) = ↑(offspringSeqLaw μ)

    The iid sequence law is shift invariant.

    MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (ω : ℕ → ℕ) (i : ℕ) => ↑↑(ω i) - 1) ↑(offspringSeqLaw μ) = ↑(Probability.RealWalk.iidLaw (realOffspringStepLaw μ))

    The walk (ω i - 1)_i of offspringSeqLaw μ is the iid real walk with step law realOffspringStepLaw μ.
