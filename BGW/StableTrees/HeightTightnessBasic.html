    Oscillation observables for the discrete forest height #

    The height is interpolated before rescaling. Its modulus is measurable as a lower semicontinuous functional of the continuous height path. The final adjacent height jump is bounded by the modulus on the longer horizon T + 1, and the modulus is monotone in its time scale. A generic dominated convergence lemma for events that eventually fail almost surely is also recorded here.
