    The reflected occupation convolution #

    Independence at the exponential clock reduces reflection from a level to the convolution of the supremum tail with the exponential drawdown density.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.
