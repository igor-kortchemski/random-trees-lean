    Absolute continuity of the stable scale series #

    Nonnegative derivatives give integrability at zero. The series at |q| dominates signed rates, and the fundamental theorem of calculus identifies the integral.

    Reference: A. E. Kyprianou, Fluctuations of Lévy Processes with Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2014, Chapter 8.
