    The auxiliary clock germ event #

    The extended occupation limsup is monotone on every path. This makes immediate positivity a raw-prefix event at every positive horizon, hence an event of the right-continuous germ. The potential has already been constructed independently of the zero-one argument.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
