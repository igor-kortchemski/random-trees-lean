    Boundary potential and ascending ladder Laplace functional #

    The boundary potential identifies the ladder semigroup rate exactly as supLadderExponent, with normalization one, at all nonnegative parameters. The proof uses cumulative Tonelli, compact square-mean occupation error and half-rate tail bounds.
