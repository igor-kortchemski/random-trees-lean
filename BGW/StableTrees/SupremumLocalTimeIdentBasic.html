    Public occupation sequence and totalized supremum clock #

    The auxiliary fast sequence supplies existence. The extended limsup retains pathwise monotonicity even on exceptional paths where the real limsup has its default value.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
