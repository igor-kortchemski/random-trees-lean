    Fixed-time height approximation #

    Reversal identifies occupation approximations outside the countable set of jumps. Prefix events transport along the equality of restricted-path laws, yielding both almost sure convergence along the public sequence and convergence in measure.

    heightApprox ε f t = supOccupation ε (reversedAt t f) t
