    Joint convergence of the forest walk, height and contour #

    On every compact horizon, the padded contour is a uniformly vanishing perturbation of the interpolated height. Slutsky's theorem therefore upgrades the joint walk/height limit to the joint walk/height/contour limit, with the same continuous height in the last two coordinates.
