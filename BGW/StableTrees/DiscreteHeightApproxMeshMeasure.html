    Lebesgue measure of occupation cells #

    The floor fibers are disjoint half-open cells of equal length. The only extra piece after the discrete mesh endpoint is the final incomplete cell.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteHeightApprox.measureReal_meshCells {n : ℕ} (hn : 0 < n) (J : Finset ℕ) :
    MeasureTheory.volume.real (⋃ j ∈ J, Set.Ico (↑j / ↑n) ((↑j + 1) / ↑n)) = ↑J.card / ↑n

    A finite union of distinct mesh cells has length equal to its cardinal divided by n.
