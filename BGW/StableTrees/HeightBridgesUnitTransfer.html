    Transfer of height approximation limits to the unit excursion law #

    The unit prefix density transfers existence of an approximation limit. A measurable prefix representative is extracted from convergence in measure before changing the law.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.unitPrefix_absolutelyContinuous_excursion {θ : ℝ} (hθ : 1 < θ ∧ θ ≤ 2) (t : NNReal) (ht0 : 0 < t) (ht1 : t < 1) :
    (unitPrefix θ t).AbsolutelyContinuous (MeasureTheory.Measure.map (excursionRestrict t) ((excursionMeasure θ).restrict {e : Excursion | t < e.lifetime}))

    Unit prefixes are absolutely continuous with respect to the corresponding surviving excursion prefixes.

    ∃ (L : Excursion → ℝ), Measurable L ∧ MeasureTheory.TendstoInMeasure (↑(unitExcursionLaw θ)) (fun (ε : ℝ) (e : Excursion) => heightApprox ε e.path t) (nhdsWithin 0 (Set.Ioi 0)) L

    Existence of an excursion approximation limit implies existence of a unit-law limit. The excursion convergence hypothesis remains an independent obligation.
