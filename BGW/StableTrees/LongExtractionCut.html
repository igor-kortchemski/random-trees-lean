    Stopped-cut convergence for the first long block #

    Endpoint convergence and joint continuity of moving stopped cuts identify the extracted path. The modification of the last step costs at most one spatial mesh.

    Stopped-cut convergence: the whole triple (g_n, d_n, cut_n) converges, in ℝ × ℝ × D T ℝ, for every cutoff T at which the limit cut is continuous. The cut is the translated block path, extended by zero.
