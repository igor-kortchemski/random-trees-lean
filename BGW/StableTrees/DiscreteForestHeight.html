    The deterministic shifted-height identity #

    Separate records preceding the shift from records inside the shifted interval. The former attain their final count at the first minimum of the intervening walk.

    source
    theorem BGW.StableTrees.DiscreteForest.shiftedHeight_eq (ω : ℕ → ℕ) (m k : ℕ) :
    shiftedHeight ω m k = ↑(forestHeight (shiftSeq m ω) k)

    (Finding 3, deterministic) H_{m+k} - min_{m≤j≤m+k} H_j = H_k of the shifted sequence, for every ω, m, k.
