    Erased height and the reversed supremum clock #

    Surplus mass in a terminal source window remains after erasure. Its ancestral heights bound the surviving support from below. Only continuity of the reversed supremum clock is used; continuity of the height process is unnecessary.

    (eraseMass m ρ) (Set.Ioi R) = 0
