    Forest masses and prefix weights #

    Canonical forest mass, inclusive tail and exact-prefix weight definitions. The probability identities retain extinction and admissibility hypotheses explicitly.

    Reference: J.-F. Le Gall, Random trees and applications, Probab. Surveys 2 (2005), Section 1.
