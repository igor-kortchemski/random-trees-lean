    Discounted occupation bounds before recurrence #

    The reflected resolvent is an unnormalised time integral. Its uniform bound controls first moments of the cumulative occupations at an arbitrary positive rate.

    References #

    • J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.
