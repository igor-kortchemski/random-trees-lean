    Compact occupation errors and their resolvent martingale #

    The occupation Cauchy construction uses the reflected resolvent at rate one. It builds on BGW.StableTrees.SupremumPotential, BGW.StableTrees.SupremumReflectedFamily and the Doob estimates of BGW.Probability.DoobL2Resolvent.

    References #

    • [J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV]
