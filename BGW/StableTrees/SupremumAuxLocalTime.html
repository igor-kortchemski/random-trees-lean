    Auxiliary continuous local time at the supremum #

    The occupation limit supAuxLimit of BGW.StableTrees.SupremumCauchy is a continuous monotone clock, positive immediately and recurrent. Its atomless Stieltjes measure is supported on supremum contact. Its strict inverse and ladder height satisfy simultaneous pathwise geometry, with fixed-level a.e. measurability.

    The mean and conditional discounted potentials are proved first from supOccupationResolvent (BGW.StableTrees.SupremumPotential) and supAuxLimit, using ordinary cumulative-integral formulas and half-rate growth bounds. The ENNReal germ helper is defined locally in this module. Scaling and Blumenthal's law then prove positivity and recurrence. The proof uses no identification of the public local time, all-path inverse measurability or stopping-time result.

    The conditional potential is a deterministic-time identity. Consumers must not substitute a random time into that almost-everywhere equality without a separate stopping argument.
