    Supremum occupation approximations #

    Deterministic bounds and fixed-time measurability for the running supremum and its occupation approximations. The simultaneous occupation-sequence existence theorem and the ascending local-time construction are not established here.

    These are preliminary objects for Duquesne and Le Gall, Random Trees, Lévy Processes and Spatial Branching Processes, Astérisque 281 (2002), Section 1.1.
