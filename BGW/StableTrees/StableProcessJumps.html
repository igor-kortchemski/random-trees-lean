    Negative jumps and the offspring step-path bound #

    Negation preserves J1 distance. An offspring walk has steps at least -1, so its normalized step paths have negative jumps bounded by the reciprocal normalizer, including at the horizon. The jump functional is that of Billingsley, Convergence of Probability Measures, 2nd ed., 1999, Section 13.
