    Universal height sequence: definitions and deterministic properties #

    The sequence interface records rational-age and almost-everywhere-age convergence under all laws. The totalized limsup is measurable, nonnegative, and depends literally on the prefix.

    source
    def BGW.StableTrees.IsUniversalHeightSeq (θ : ℝ) (ε : ℕ → ℝ) :
    Prop

    ε is positive, strictly decreasing to 0, and along it the approximations converge almost surely at every rational time: to H02's height under the stable law, and to some limit under N on {q < ζ} and under the unit law at ages q < 1; the same holds at Lebesgue-almost every age (needed by H04's locality at the random ages q - g).
