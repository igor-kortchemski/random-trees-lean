    Strict hitting times of the totalized clock #

    An increasing crossing predicate is detected by rational times after the crossing. This retains raw prefix measurability without assuming continuity of the extended clock.

    Reference: J. Bertoin, Lévy Processes, Cambridge University Press, 1996, Chapter IV.

    source
    noncomputable def BGW.StableTrees.supHitting (θ : ℝ) (z : NNReal) (f : Skorokhod.DLoc) :
    ENNReal

    Strict hitting time a_z = inf{t | p(t) > z} of the ENNReal clock (the stopping-time representative of the inverse; ⊤ if never).
