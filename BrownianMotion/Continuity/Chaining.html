    Chaining #

    Given finite sets C n that cover a set A at scales ε n, chaining approximates a point x ∈ C k successively by points of C (k - 1), C (k - 2), ..., C 0. Bounds on the increments of a process along these chains give bounds on its suprema over A.

    Main definitions #

    • Finset.nearestPt s x: a point of the finite set s that is closest to x.
    • chainingSeq C x k n: the approximation of x at level n. It is x for k ≤ n, and the nearest point in C n of the approximation at level n + 1 for n < k.

    Main statements #

    • edist_chainingSeq_le_sum: if each C i is an ε i-cover of A contained in A and x ∈ C k, the distance between x and its approximation at level m ≤ k is at most ∑ i ∈ Finset.range (k - m), ε (m + i).
    • edist_chainingSeq_pow_two_le: for the scales ε₀ * 2⁻¹ ^ i, the approximations at level m of x ∈ C k and y ∈ C n are at distance at most edist x y + ε₀ * 4 * 2⁻¹ ^ m.
    • iSup_edist_le_iSup_edist_chainingSeq: the supremum of edist (X s) (X t) over the pairs of points of C k at distance at most δ is bounded by the same supremum for their approximations at level m, plus twice the supremum of edist (X s) (X (chainingSeq C s k m)).

    References #

    • [R. Vershynin, High-dimensional probability, Chapter 8][vershynin2018high]
    • [M. Talagrand, Upper and lower bounds for stochastic processes][talagrand2014]
