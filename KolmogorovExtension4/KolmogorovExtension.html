    Kolmogorov extension theorem #

    Given a projective family of finite measures P J on the finite products Π j : J, α j, we construct its projective limit, a measure on Π i, α i, when the spaces α i are second-countable and completely pseudo-metrizable (for example Polish spaces).

    If the measures P J are inner regular with respect to compact closed sets, the additive content projectiveFamilyContent hP on the measurable cylinders is inner regular with respect to a compact system of square cylinders, hence it is σ-additive and extends to a measure.

    Main definitions #

    • projectiveLimit P hP: the projective limit of a projective family of finite measures.

    Main statements #

    • projectiveFamilyContent_sigma_additive_of_innerRegular: the content projectiveFamilyContent hP is σ-additive if the measures P J are inner regular with respect to compact closed sets.
    • isProjectiveLimit_projectiveLimit: Kolmogorov extension theorem, projectiveLimit P hP is the projective limit of P.
