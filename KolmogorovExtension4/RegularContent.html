    σ-additivity of inner regular contents #

    An additive content on a ring of sets which is inner regular with respect to a compact system contained in the ring is σ-additive.

    Main statements #

    • tendsto_zero_of_innerRegular_addContent: such a content is continuous at ∅.
    • addContent_iUnion_eq_sum_of_innerRegular: such a content is σ-additive, if it takes finite values.

    Let m be an additive content on a ring of sets R, inner regular with respect to a compact system C ⊆ R: every set of R can be approximated from inside by sets of C. Then m is continuous at ∅: if s is a decreasing sequence of sets of R with empty intersection, then m (s n) tends to 0.
