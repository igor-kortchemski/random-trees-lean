    A compact system of square cylinders #

    Compact closed square cylinders form a compact system (isCompactSystem_compactClosedSquareCylinders), hence so do the countable intersections of finite unions of them. We show that every cylinder with a compact closed base belongs to that compact system. This is used in the proof of the Kolmogorov extension theorem, to approximate measurable cylinders from inside by sets of a compact system.

    Main statements #

    • isCompactSystem_countableInfClosure_supClosure_compactClosedSquareCylinders: countable intersections of finite unions of compact closed square cylinders form a compact system.
    • cylinder_mem_countableInfClosure_supClosure: in a product of second-countable R₁ spaces, a cylinder with a compact closed base is a countable intersection of finite unions of compact closed square cylinders.
