The reusable BGW foundations now include the generation process conditional on its entire
finite history, exponential tilting, geometric/uniform plane trees, discrete contours, fringe
blocks and the exact uniform-vertex fringe identity. The unrooted GHP quotient is a Polish
metric space with compactness and tightness transfer to GH. The deterministic real-tree
criterion and the scalar stable toolkit are integrated; their limits are described below.
P9 `cor:cyclicshift` and `lem:forest_estimates` are **Proved in atomic form**; the other 33 labeled P9 results remain
**Unproved in this library**. This does not describe their status in companion projects.
A completed entry needs a proved export or checked specialization, source correspondence,
and relevant build and axiom evidence. A conditional helper does not close a source result.

The P9 probability results use critical offspring with $\mu_1\ne1$, stable index $\alpha\in(1,2]$, and conditioning through admissible sizes. These are not assumptions of the deterministic library. Preserve positive-probability conditions, lattice endpoints and each statement's additional assumptions. The source defines microscopicity `(M)` at 215–225, structural condition `(S)` at 239–257, and pair non-clustering `(C)` at 275–295; `(S)` is a sufficient route to `(M)`, not an additional universal premise.

## Implemented foundations

All exports below are available through [BGW.lean](BGW.lean). Finite-tree operations belong to `BGW.PlaneTree`, the new carrier to `BGW.LocallyFinite`, forest operations to `BGW.PlaneForest`, and `IsTreeExcursion` to `BGW`.

- [Tree/CycleLemma.lean](BGW/Tree/CycleLemma.lean) defines `bridgeWords n`, the words of length $n$ with entries at least $-1$ and sum $-1$. - [LocallyFinite/SizeConditioning.lean](BGW/LocallyFinite/SizeConditioning.lean) defines `sizeEvent n = {T | FiniteVertices T ∧ {u | Present T u}.ncard = n}`, the event that the tree is finite with exactly $n$ vertices, leaves and root included. For the conditioned law `(bienaymeLaw μ)[|sizeEvent n]` (Mathlib's `ProbabilityTheory.cond`), `natCast_mul_bienaymeLaw_sizeEvent` gives the weighted Kemperman–Dwass identity $n\,P(|T|=n)=\sum_{x\ \text{bridge}}\mathrm{walkWeight}(x)$ (`Tree/CycleLemma.lean`), `bienaymeLaw_sizeEvent_eq_tsum_excursions` and `bienaymeLaw_sizeEvent_ne_zero_iff_excursion` restate the mass and the admissibility through tree excursions (`Tree/WalkWeight.lean`), `isProbabilityMeasure_cond_sizeEvent` holds at an admissible size, `cond_sizeEvent_self` says it gives mass one to the size event, `cond_sizeEvent_eq_zero` records that it is the zero measure otherwise, and `cond_sizeEvent_singleton` gives the atom at `t.toLocallyFinite`. The source's statements that the set of admissible sizes is infinite for critical laws and the filter of large admissible sizes need critical hypotheses and are not part of this module.
